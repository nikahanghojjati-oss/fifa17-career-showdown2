DEVICE CHECK

Plays a Chamber Men kick, a Russian Roulette turn, a Special Player reveal and a Ball Game spin at each 3D quality level, lightest first. Each screen then gets the best level it keeps smooth at 60 fps, with no stutter, so a lighter screen can run higher than a heavier one. A screen that is not smooth is played once more before it counts, and stops climbing at the first level that is still not smooth. It only ever raises Auto above the level your graphics chip starts at. It takes a few minutes. Keep this tab open and in front. Nothing in your Showdown changes.
