# Screen 11 — Trophy Room / `trophyRoom`

Status: ACTIVE SCREEN CONTRACT

Source anchor: current `main` at `cef2e101f23fd8cb777f71950bac8f0f8d9f2c7b` and established career/trophy analytics authority.

## Purpose

Trophy Room is the private rivalry prestige archive. It celebrates earned honours without copying official trophy forms, club crests or public leaderboard conventions.

## Required current modes

Cover:

- empty state;
- unresolved historical identity notice;
- career summary;
- career table/context where surfaced;
- Manager Cabinets;
- trophy shelves;
- supporting achievement statistics;
- all-time records.

## Manager cabinet treatment

Each manager receives equal cabinet geometry and identical information capacity.

Canonical proposal examples preserve Manager 1 = Daniel and Manager 2 = Nik. Live identity and trophy counts remain product-derived authority.

Primary cabinet values:

- total trophies;
- career points.

Secondary achievements may include season wins, perfect seasons, 100-point seasons, 100-goal seasons and safe signings according to current analytics.

A manager with fewer trophies remains fully readable and does not receive a visually broken/empty cabinet.

## Trophy language

Use original symbolic trophy silhouettes/icons for Champions League, League and Main Cup categories. The shapes should be generic enough to remain rights-safe and must not trace or imitate official UEFA/domestic trophy silhouettes closely.

Do not use official club crests.

All-time records read as private record plaques/cards. Avoid podiums, global rank numbers or language suggesting public competition outside the two-manager/local career data.

## Empty and unresolved-identity states

Empty state uses an original shelf/outline motif with explanatory copy. Do not show fake trophies.

Unresolved identity is an attention state, not a destructive error. It must explain the limitation clearly and preserve access to available data without visually implying an automatic merge.

## Accessibility and QA

- every trophy icon has adjacent text or accessible label;
- trophy count is never conveyed only by repeated decorative icons;
- equal cabinet geometry does not imply equal outcomes;
- unresolved identity is text-explicit;
- empty state is distinct from loading/error;
- all-time records remain private/local in wording and visual convention;
- focus remains visible on navigation/identity-resolution controls;
- reduced motion removes decorative shine without affecting data.
