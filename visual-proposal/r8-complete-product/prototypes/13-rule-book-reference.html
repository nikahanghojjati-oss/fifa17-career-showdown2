Editorial behavior

Long copy stays at an ordinary readable line length. Gold is reserved for numbering, canonical values and current-section emphasis rather than turning every sentence into an accent. Character art is omitted because this is a reference/reading surface.

Historical Rule Book copy can lag connected/shared capabilities. R8 styles product-owned copy but does not silently rewrite semantic authority. Final reconciliation flags stale wording to the senior developer.
