Purpose: translate verified club history, place and broad football-design context into original Showdown identity direction. This ledger is authoring evidence only. ## Angers

Production name: `Angers`

Facts: Angers SCO traces its foundation to 1919, originally as Sporting Club du Crédit de l'Ouest before becoming Sporting Club de l'Ouest. The club is rooted in Angers and the Anjou area.

Source:
- https://www.angers-sco.fr/histoire/

Safe design inputs: early-20th-century western French club; black/white/gold palette; Anjou/Loire locality; restrained river/stone/grid geometry can distinguish the identity without reproducing heraldry.

Exclude: copied SCO lettering arrangement; official shield/crest contour; Anjou/Angers civic heraldry.

## Bastia

Production name: `Bastia`

Facts: SC Bastia's official history traces the club to 1905 in Bastia. Early football activity is associated with Place Saint-Nicolas. The club is strongly tied to Corsica and the island city of Bastia.

Sources:
- https://sc-bastia.corsica/blogs/histoire
- https://sc-bastia.corsica/pages/le-club

Safe design inputs: early-20th-century Corsican island club; blue/light/dark palette; island/port/plaza geometry; compact Mediterranean proportions.

Exclude: Moor's head; Corsican flag/heraldry; copied official shield or SCB wordmark.

Safe design inputs: merger-derived identity; Normandy/locality; blue/red/light palette; two-root convergence and masonry/grid geometry.

## Guingamp

Production name: `Guingamp`

Facts: En Avant de Guingamp traces its foundation to 1912, created by secular schoolteachers around an educational and sporting project. The club is famous for the scale of its support relative to the small Breton town of Guingamp.

Sources:
- https://www.eaguingamp.com/le-club/histoire/
- https://www.eaguingamp.com/le-club/

Safe design inputs: community/education origin; small-town collective identity; Brittany; red/black/light palette; people-grid or converging-community geometry.

Exclude: Breton heraldry/ermine; copied EAG shield/wordmark; literal school insignia.

## Lille

Production name: `Lille`

Facts: LOSC was formed in 1944 through the union of Olympique Lillois and SC Fives. Olympique Lillois had won the first French professional championship in 1932-33. The club is rooted in Lille and northern France.

Sources:
- https://www.losc.fr/club/histoire
- https://www.losc.fr/actualites/2024-11-25/80-ans-losc

Safe design inputs: post-war two-root merger; northern industrial/metropolitan locality; red/navy/light palette; dual-lane convergence and robust geometric structure.

Exclude: dog/mastiff; copied official hex/crest; official LOSC lettering.

## Lorient

Production name: `Lorient`

Source:
- https://fclorient.bzh/le-club/histoire/

Safe design inputs: dock/fishing-worker origin; port; orange/black/light palette; dock rails, harbor lanes and wave geometry kept abstract.

Exclude: fish/hake symbol; copied official shield/roundel; maritime civic heraldry.

## Nantes

Safe design inputs: five-root convergence; collective-flow identity; yellow/green/dark palette; five-lane convergence, flowing geometry and western/Loire locality.

## Rennes

Source:

Safe design inputs: student origin plus early merger; Brittany/Rennes; red/black/light palette; river/academic-grid and converging geometry.

## Saint-Étienne

Production name: `Saint-Étienne`

Facts: the club's roots date to 1912 in a sporting organization created by employees of the Casino retail group; the professional AS Saint-Étienne identity developed in the 1930s. Green is deeply associated with the club's history. Saint-Étienne is an industrial city.

Source:
- https://www.asse.fr/fr/club/histoire

Safe design inputs: worker/employee origin; industrial city; green/light/gold palette; factory-grid, rail or labor-cooperative geometry kept abstract.

Exclude: Casino corporate marks; official star/crest; Saint-Étienne civic heraldry; sponsor/corporate devices.

## Toulouse

Production name: `Toulouse`

Facts: the current Toulouse FC lineage dates to 1970, initially as Union Sportive de Toulouse before adopting the Toulouse FC identity. The club represents Toulouse and uses violet/white as central colors. This current club should not be conflated with the earlier Toulouse FC founded in 1937.

Sources:
- https://www.toulousefc.com/club/histoire
- https://www.toulousefc.com/

Safe design inputs: modern 1970 identity; Toulouse/Garonne locality; violet/light/gold palette; river/radial/brick-city geometry kept abstract.

Exclude: Occitan cross; Toulouse civic heraldry; copied official TFC shield/monogram; visible 1937 as current-club founding identity.
