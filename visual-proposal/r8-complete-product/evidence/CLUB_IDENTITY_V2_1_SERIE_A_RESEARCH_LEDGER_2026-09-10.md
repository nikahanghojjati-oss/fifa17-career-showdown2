## Atalanta

Production name: `Atalanta`

Facts: Atalanta's official history dates the club to 1907 in Bergamo. The modern black-and-blue identity followed the 1920 merger with Bergamasca, whose colors included blue and white while Atalanta had used black and white. The club name comes from the mythological Atalanta.

Sources:
- https://en.atalanta.it/news/17-10-2023-happy-116th-birthday-atalanta
- https://www.atalanta.it/club/storia/

Safe design inputs: early-20th-century Bergamo club; two-root merger; blue/black contrast; Bergamo foothill/ridge context can become abstract stepped geometry.

Exclude: mythological woman/profile; copied official oval; official Atalanta wordmark; Bergamo civic heraldry.

## Bologna

Production name: `Bologna`

Facts: Bologna FC's official history says the club was founded on 3 October 1909 at the Ronzani beer house by an international group including students. The first red-and-blue colors came from Arrigo Gradi's Swiss college quartered shirt before the club adopted vertical stripes.

Source:
- https://www.bolognafc.it/en/history/

Safe design inputs: early-20th-century international/student origin; red/blue; quartered-to-striped kit history can inspire an original transitional field pattern.

Exclude: copied official oval/roundel; BFC 1909 lettering arrangement; Bologna civic heraldry.

- https://www.legaseriea.it/serie-a/news/la-fiorentina-compie-100-anni

Safe design inputs: interwar merger identity; Florence/Tuscany; purple/light/gold palette; two-root convergence plus refined geometric/Renaissance-proportion influence kept generic.

Exclude: fleur-de-lis; Florence civic lily; copied official diamond/shield; official ACF lettering.

## History-complexity policy reinforced by Serie A

Napoli, Palermo and Udinese all demonstrate why V2.1 cannot treat a single founding year as runtime identity data. Their football histories contain predecessor organizations, reorganizations or multiple meaningful institutional milestones. The badge can inherit era and locality without printing or persisting one potentially reductive date.

Several clubs also have very distinctive official symbols whose removal alone would not make a near-copy safe. V2.1 therefore starts from factual context and original geometry rather than simplifying the official crest.
