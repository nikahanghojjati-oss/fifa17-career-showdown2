## Method

For each supported club:

1. preserve the production club name exactly;
2. record a small factual history/place set from official or high-quality historical sources;
3. translate facts into generic visual-language categories;
4. explicitly record distinctive official devices that must not be used as the recognition shortcut;
5. send only compact abstract descriptor fields to the runtime identity catalog.

The desired outcome is `club character -> original Showdown identity`, never `official badge -> altered copy`.

## Burnley

Production name: `Burnley`

Facts: Burnley FC's official history says Burnley Rovers began as a rugby club and switched codes to become Burnley Football Club in 1882. It moved to Turf Moor shortly afterward at the invitation of the town cricket club and was a founder member of the Football League. Early colors differed before the later claret-and-blue identity.

Sources:
- https://www.burnleyfootballclub.com/burnley-fc-club-history
- https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4592046/what-is-the-history-behind-every-premier-league-clubs-name

Safe design inputs: late-Victorian multi-sport origin; Lancashire town/ground heritage; claret-and-sky-blue balance; traditional compact shield with field structure unlike West Ham.

Exclude: copied official heraldic devices; bees/lions/storks or municipal heraldry copied from Burnley marks; exact official shield arrangement.
