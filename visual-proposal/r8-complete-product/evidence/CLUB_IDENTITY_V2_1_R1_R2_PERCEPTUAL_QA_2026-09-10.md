## Face-value perceptual ruling

### Athletic Club — PASS

R2 moves away from the prior tall red/white striped shield. The new oval/diagonal field and abstract Nervión/industrial bridge language reads as a distinct Showdown identity. It does not depend on official crest geometry or a copied internal emblem.

### Sporting Gijón — PASS

R2 replaces the more familiar striped-shield family with a pennant/diagonal coastal composition and abstract wave treatment. It remains recognizably red/white and coastal without tracing the official crest.

### Bayern Munich — PASS

R2 removes the familiar round/circular family. The red/blue faceted hex, quartered field and abstract starburst create strong differentiation while preserving the club-associated palette.

### Inter Milan — PASS WITH WATCH NOTE

R2 removes the circular monogram family and replaces it with a wide engineered blue/black composition using an abstract beam device. The central geometry can read as letter-like at a glance, but it is generated as an abstract beam device and does not reproduce the official FCIM monogram. Keep on the final 42–48px confusion review list.

### Napoli — PASS

R2 removes the circular/roundel family and visible initial dependency. The coastal hex plus radial/wave language preserves sky-blue identity while clearly separating the mark from the official N roundel.

### Nice — PASS

R2 uses a red/black angular pennant with generic ridge/coastal geometry and no copied eagle/device. It remains high-contrast and club-associated without official crest shorthand.

### Paris Saint-Germain — PASS WITH WATCH NOTE

R2 removes the roundel family and uses a split-panel metropolitan composition with abstract bridge/grid geometry. The red/blue/white hierarchy remains familiar as club-associated color language, while the silhouette and device are original. Keep on the final small-size confusion review list.
