# R8.33 Reference-Faithful Replica Target

Status: ACTIVE OWNER-DIRECTION RECORD / PROPOSAL ONLY / NOT PRODUCTION

## Owner direction captured 2026-09-11

The supplied Home, Select League and Club Assignment images are now the visual target for these three proposal screens. They are `REFERENCE_ONLY / OWNER VISUAL-LANGUAGE APPROVED`, not runtime/product authority.

The implementation objective is no longer merely "FIFA-17 inspired" or a lightweight black/gold approximation. The proposal should reproduce the reference composition as closely as practical with real HTML/CSS/JS/SVG and bounded source art while preserving the actual product contracts.

## Required visual characteristics

- persistent CM17 black/gold top shell with a strong left brand mark, compact navigation, utility cluster and small handwritten motto;
- warm, full-bleed stadium atmosphere with bright floodlights, dense crowd texture, black foreground panels and narrow gold rails;
- large Daniel/Nik character presence on wide desktop, with rim-lit hair/shoulders and page-specific pose intent;
- handwritten gold manager name/titles plus compact uppercase personality lines;
- bold distressed/brush-like gold page titles with crown language and condensed uppercase support copy;
- metallic near-black action surfaces; selected/primary surfaces become bright gold rather than simply changing border color;
- purpose-built tile/icon compositions rather than generic cards;
- Home keeps the six real product actions and media surface, but should feel like the reference Rivalry Headquarters composition;
- League Wheel keeps the real five-option wheel and product-owned selection state, but should visually read like the reference centered golden wheel stage with Daniel left and Nik right;
- Club Assignment keeps the real reveal lifecycle, persistent club result authority and controls, but should visually read like the reference two-manager pack theatre with progress steps, central rivalry VS, lower locked-club deck and dominant Open/Confirm action;
- tablet/mobile intentionally simplify the cinematic art instead of shrinking the desktop composition until it collides.

## Practical-replica boundary

The following remain real components and must not be flattened into an image: navigation, Home action tiles, media controls, league wheel/hit area, spin/back controls, Club Assignment packs/reveal state, progress state, locked rivalry deck, confirm/open/back controls, status text and responsive layout.

Character/source artwork may be raster. The stadium may be procedural CSS/SVG until a separately accepted original environment source asset materially improves fidelity. Text and product labels remain DOM-owned.

No official club crest, official league logo, proprietary EA/FIFA artwork or copied proprietary font is introduced. Reference logos are translated into original symbolic/typographic placeholders while the product's five canonical league names remain unchanged.
