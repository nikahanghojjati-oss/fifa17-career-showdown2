## Bayern Munich

Production name: `Bayern Munich`

Facts: FC Bayern's official history dates foundation to 27 February 1900 at Café Gisela in Munich by a group led by Franz John. In 1906 Bayern joined Münchner Sport-Club while retaining independence and adopted red shorts/white shirts from MSC, colors that remained central to the club.

Sources:
- https://fcbayern.com/en/club/history/mile-stones/milestones-in-bayern-s-history
- https://fcbayern.com/en/club/history/mile-stones/1900-to-1932-success-from-the-start

Safe design inputs: turn-of-century Munich independence/rebellion origin; red/white core with blue support; elegant Bavarian/metropolitan geometric rhythm that does not reproduce regional heraldry.

Exclude: Bavarian lozenges arranged like the official crest; copied circular wordmark; official FCB monogram; state/civic heraldry.

## Borussia Dortmund

Production name: `Borussia Dortmund`

Facts: BVB's official history dates foundation to 19 December 1909 at `Zum Wildschütz` near Borsigplatz by members of the Catholic Trinity parish youth group who wanted an independent football club. Official material also notes that many of the young men were steelworkers and miners in Dortmund's industrial north. The name `Borussia` was inspired by a local brewery name. Modern club identity is black/yellow; early colors differed.

Sources:
- https://www.bvb.de/de/en/borussia-dortmund/history/decades/1909.html
- https://www.bvb.de/de/en/news/news-overview/news.html/eng/News/Overview/It-all-started-with-an-act-of-rebellion.html
- https://service.bvb.de/docs/en/geschichte-des-bvb

Safe design inputs: early-20th-century industrial-worker/rebellion origin; Borsigplatz circular/road geometry can inspire a generic radial grid; yellow/black high-contrast field; energetic working-city structure.

Exclude: official BVB circle/wordmark; exact Borsigplatz/star treatment used by club projects; brewery logo; municipal heraldry.

## Bayer Leverkusen

## Mainz 05

Production name: `Mainz 05`

Facts: Mainz 05's official chronicle says the currently accepted founding date is 16 March 1905, while pre-war sources named 27 March 1905. The club explicitly acknowledges the discrepancy. The earliest club was 1. Mainzer Fußballclub Hassia 1905, founded around Café Neuf.

Source:
- https://www.mainz05.de/chronik

Safe design inputs: early-20th-century Rhine-city club; red/light/dark palette; circular/river-curve geometry may be used, but without imitating the distinctive official `05` mark. Founding-date ambiguity remains authoring metadata only.

Exclude: copied `05` crest; exact circular official silhouette; visible precise founding date as recognition mechanism; municipal heraldry.

## Hamburg

Production name: `Hamburg`

Facts: HSV's official history explains that the modern club emerged from the 1919 union of Hamburger SV von 1888, SC Germania von 1887 and FC Falke 06. The club later adopted 29 September 1887 as its official birthday in recognition of Germania. Official material also links Hamburg football development to the city's role as a major trading/seaport hub.

Sources:
- https://www.hsv.de/en/news/the-founding-of-hsv
- https://www.hsv.de/en/our-hsv/about-hsv/history-of-the-club/hsv-history/founding-fathers-the-early-years-of-hsv

Safe design inputs: multi-club union; Hanseatic/seaport/trading-city context; blue/light/dark palette; three-part convergence plus maritime grid/horizon abstraction.

Exclude: official diamond; Blue Peter signal reproduction; copied city red/white heraldic treatment; HSV wordmark.

## Ambiguity and corporate-origin policy

This league reinforces two important V2.1 rules.

First, history may be institutionally complex or date-ambiguous. Mainz and Werder explicitly preserve founding-date uncertainty, while Hamburg has a multi-club lineage and adopted an older predecessor date as its birthday. The renderer therefore must not need a founding year.

Second, several clubs have strong corporate/works relationships, including Bayer Leverkusen, Wolfsburg, RB Leipzig and modern Ingolstadt. The historical relationship may inform generic industrial/modern geometry, but corporate logos and protected brand devices are prohibited. Club character cannot be reduced to a sponsor/company mark.
