The game: a private two-player FIFA 17 Career Mode tracker (Daniel and Nik). Each season, before the transfer window, each manager spins a 7-chamber revolver (fires exactly 1 in 7). A fired bullet hits one of the 15 players on that manager's squad board (11 starters + 4 subs, equal chance) and he releases that player in his FIFA 17 save. Each manager also has a Special Player (a random top FIFA 17 high-potential player, revealed after Club Assignment).

Nik's rules, which the code must follow exactly:
- A board is always full: 11 starters and 4 subs. A bullet always hits a player, never an empty slot.
- Season 1 board = the club's FIFA 17 squad: formation starting XI + the 4 highest-rated non-starters. Each club also has 3 hidden reserves (the next 3 highest-rated).
- The Special Player is never on a board, bench or reserve list, so he is never hit. If he belongs to the club he is skipped and the next player takes his place.
- From season 2: the player shot last season leaves for good. Transfer War signings that were NOT guessed (kept) join by overall and position: a signing starts only in a slot of his line (CB with CBs, ST with forwards...), replacing the weakest compatible starter only when strictly better; the replaced starter goes to the bench if he beats the weakest sub, otherwise to the reserves. A guessed (released) signing never joins.
- A shot player's place is filled by a fitting kept signing, else the best-fitting bench player moves up and the best reserve fills the bench. Reserves used up: the club's next best database player. The roulette is NEVER skipped: a club that truly runs out of players shows an empty slot on its board, and a fired bullet only ever hits a real player, never an empty slot.
- Both phones must always show the same boards, spins and results (all seeded from the Showdown id; no Firebase reads or writes for the board itself, except one Transfer Challenge read per missing season).
