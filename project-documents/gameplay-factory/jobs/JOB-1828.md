## The bug
JOB-1812 (PR #842) makes the free-limit message show as soon as Home opens. The r76 gallery (JOB-1819, PR #855, folder
`test-results/r76-gallery/` on branch `codex/job-1819-r76-gallery`) found two phone problems at 393x852:
- `home-free-limit-phone-393x852.webp`: the notice **covers the soundtrack controls** on Home.
- `connect-players-free-limit-phone-393x852.webp`: a **clipped strip of the Home notice shows through** above the navigation on
  Connect Players.
Nik's rule: buttons, scoreboards and messages must never cover one another at any size. Desktop looked right; check it stays so.

## What to do
1. **Reproduce first** on the 1812 head with a small Playwright audit `tests/browser/free-limit-notice-layout-audit.cjs`
   (use `tests/support/chromium-runtime.cjs`; force the free-limit state the way `tests/contracts/free-limit-home-message-contracts.cjs`
   does). At each size **360x640, 375x553, 390x844, 393x852, 430x932, 740x360, 844x390, 768x1024, 1280x800, 1920x1080**, on Home and
   on Connect Players: the notice's box must not overlap any visible button or control (check with `getBoundingClientRect` and
   `document.elementFromPoint` at the centre of every soundtrack control and navigation button: it must return that control),
   and on Connect Players no part of the Home notice may be visible. Save a screenshot per size. It must fail on the 1812 head.
2. **Find the cause** (file:line): most likely the notice is placed in a container that stays painted when Home is not the
   active screen, or it is positioned over the soundtrack row instead of taking its own space. In this app `main` is the only
   scroller; html/body never scroll.
3. **Fix** with the smallest change: the notice takes its own space in the Home flow (or the existing message slot) so nothing
   moves under it, and it is hidden whenever Home is not the active screen. Keep the exact wording and look of the notice
   (same colours, font and size). You may change only `js/homeScreensV10.js` and the one CSS rule for the notice's own
   selector in a `css/*V10.css` file. Nothing else visual.
