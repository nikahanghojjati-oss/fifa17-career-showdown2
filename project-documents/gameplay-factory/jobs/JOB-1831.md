## Why
Found by the lead's audit follow-up (`/mnt/project-files/gameplay/r76/verify/MEGA_FOLLOWUP.md`, item productionSharedCareerStart-2 F1).
In `js/productionSharedCareerStart.js`, `pcstOpenPanel` (about line 155) keeps `openPromise` until the open finishes, and nothing in
the open has a time limit: not the refresh, not the account import, not the Firestore transaction. If one of them never settles
(phone goes to sleep, network drops mid-request), `openPromise` is never cleared, the button stays on "OPENING CAREER START…" and
CONTINUE TO CAREER START stays disabled until the page is reloaded. Closing the panel with x does not clear it either.
`pcstRefresh` (about line 67) and the work queued by `pcstSerialize` (line 36) have the same problem, so REFRESH and
"I STARTED AT …" can also stay stuck.

## What to do
1. **Test first (must fail on the base).** `tests/contracts/career-start-open-timeout-contracts.cjs` (vm sandbox with the real
   controller, fake timers, a fake provider whose read never resolves; see `tests/contracts/firestore-budget-contracts.cjs` for the
   sandbox style). Assert: after the time limit the button is enabled again with its normal label, a short error is shown, and a
   second tap opens the panel when the provider now answers. Same for REFRESH and for "I STARTED AT …". Also assert a normal open
   that answers in time behaves exactly as before (same reads, same order).
2. **Fix, smallest change.** Copy the pattern of `psscWithTimeout` in `js/productionSharedSeasonCommit.js:51` (a local helper, about
   25 s). On timeout: clear `openPromise` / `refreshPromise`, let the `pcstSerialize` queue move on, call `pcstDecorateControl()`,
   and show the existing error area with "Career Start took too long. Check your connection and tap again." A late answer that
   arrives after the timeout must not overwrite a newer state (compare a request counter or the context key before applying).
   The timeout must never START a read by itself: no retry loop, no new timer that reads Firestore, no change to existing timers.
