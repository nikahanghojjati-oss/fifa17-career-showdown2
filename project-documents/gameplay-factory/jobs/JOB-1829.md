## Why
r76 must reach at least 10 two-player seasons a day (1-hour seasons) and 14 a day (30-minute seasons) on the free Firebase plan.
JOB-1825 (PR #853, report `reports/rules-cost/` on `codex/job-1825-rules-cost`) ranked every read by cost. Its top six causes all
come from one place: the Transfer Challenge screen re-reads the same six documents every time it refreshes, even when nothing
changed:
`js/productionSharedTransferChallenge.js` `pstcRefreshNow` (about line 111) → `pstcProviderOptions` (about line 90:
`setupApi.refresh()`, `careerApi.refresh()`) → `provider.read` → `stspRead` / `stspContext` in
`js/sparkSharedTransferChallenge.js` (about lines 127 and 151). The six documents are
`transferChallenges/season_{n}/roles/{role}`, `transferChallenges/season_{n}`, `careerStart/authoritative`, `sessions/{session}`,
the rivalry document and `sharedSetup/authoritative`. Each Firestore read also pays its own security-check lookups, so every
read we skip saves several. Nik's rule: cut only repeated presentation reads. Never slow or break the flow, connection,
continuity, consistency, or how Showdown results save and show on every screen.
