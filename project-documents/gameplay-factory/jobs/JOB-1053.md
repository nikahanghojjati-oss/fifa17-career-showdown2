## Bug
On a device with no career saved yet, a backup restore brings a backup career back even when the player chose **Keep current** for every item. The result is `{"gamesRestored":1}` when it should be nothing restored. The case where the device already has a career was fixed in JOB-1027; do not change it.

## Read (only these)
1. `js/restore.js`: `createCareerModeRestorePlan`, especially the `destinationIsClean` branch (lines ~140-185).
2. The repro: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nikahanghojjati-oss/fifa17-career-showdown2/qa/bug-olympiad/project-documents/gameplay-factory/sweeps/olympiad/test/codex-1006-1312-keep-empty.cjs`
3. Its fixed sibling, for comparison: `.../sweeps/olympiad/test/codex-1006-1312-keep-current.cjs`

## The change (one file, `js/restore.js`)
- Keep current always means keep what this device has, even when that is nothing. An empty device stays empty for every item where the choice is Keep current.
- DEFAULT: `destinationIsClean` may only change what happens when the choice is Use backup. It must never turn a Keep current choice into a restore.
- Use backup choices behave exactly as today.
