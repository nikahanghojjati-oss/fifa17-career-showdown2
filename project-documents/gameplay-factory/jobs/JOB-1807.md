# JOB-1807 · REVIEW SEASON button hidden on the Showdown Champion screen

## The bug
On the Showdown Champion (Final Winner) screen, the **REVIEW SEASON** button (`#completeSeason`, moved into `#v10ExitSlot`) is
`display:none` in every CE2PG run, so the player cannot review the final season from there. BACK TO SHOWDOWN HOME works.
Release r74 fixed the exits so both buttons are always reachable at 27 window sizes; something hides REVIEW SEASON again in a
shared Showdown.

## Where to start
- `js/productionSharedSeasonResultsRoute.js:44` injects a style:
  `#seasonEntry[data-shared-season-results="review"] #completeSeason{display:none!important}`.
- `js/productionSharedSeasonResults.js:135` (`pssrEntryMode`) hides `.seasonEntryActions` and sets
  `data-shared-season-results` to "review"/"entry"; line 153 renames the button.
- `js/seasonFinalV10.js:224` and `:271` move `.seasonEntryActions` into and out of the Champion screen.
Find which rule hides it on the Champion screen and make REVIEW SEASON visible there without changing the shared Season Results
review flow (where hiding `#completeSeason` during review is intended).

## Test to add (must fail on the base)
`tests/browser/champion-review-season-visible-audit.cjs`: reach the Champion screen of a finished shared Showdown the way
`tests/browser/shared-final-reconciliation-audit.cjs` or `tests/browser/shared-terminal-close-audit.cjs` do, and assert
`#completeSeason` is visible (non-zero box, not `display:none`, not covered at its centre: use `document.elementFromPoint`) at
1920x1080, 1280x650, 393x660 and 844x390, and that pressing it opens the season review.

## Proof required
New audit fails on the base and passes after; `node tests/browser/shared-terminal-close-audit.cjs`,
`node tests/browser/shared-final-reconciliation-audit.cjs`, `CMS_CHROMIUM_MULTI_CONTEXT=1 node tests/browser/shared-season-results-audit.cjs`,
`CMS_CHROMIUM_MULTI_CONTEXT=1 node tests/browser/v10-screen-flash-audit.cjs` pass. Attach before/after screenshots of the
Champion screen at 1920x1080 and 393x660 to the PR. The look of the buttons must not change; only REVIEW SEASON's visibility.

## Exact pointers (from Claude's standing-bug inventory of r75; confirm by reading)
Decision taken (Team G lead, so you don't need to ask): on the Champion screen, REVIEW SEASON must be visible and open the read-only review of the final season, exactly as release r74 made it (both exits reachable at 27 sizes). The hiding rule at `js/productionSharedSeasonResultsRoute.js:44` is correct for the entry screen during review and must keep working there. `pssrEntryMode` (`js/productionSharedSeasonResults.js:135`) leaves `data-shared-season-results="review"` set, and the button is moved into the Champion exit slot at `js/seasonFinalV10.js:282`; scope the hiding rule so it does not apply once the button is in the Champion exit slot (`#v10ExitSlot`), or reset the dataset when the Champion screen takes the button.
