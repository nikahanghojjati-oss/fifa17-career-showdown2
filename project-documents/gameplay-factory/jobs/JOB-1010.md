## Goal (Nik: the game must be smooth for 10 seasons)
Prove with plain `node` that a 10-season shared Showdown scores, totals, breaks ties and picks the final winner correctly. **This job finds bugs; it does not fix game code.**

## Rules of the game to check (Rule Book, `visual-assets/v10_1/season-results/app-shell.html` scoring panel)
Champions League winner +5, league title +3, domestic cup +1, 100 league points and/or 100 league goals +1 (max +1), top scorer and/or top assist +1 (max +1). Season maximum 11. A season with tied Showdown points is decided by league position (better position wins). A tied Showdown total at the end shows DRAW on the Final Winner.
