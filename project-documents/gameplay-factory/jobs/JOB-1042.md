## Root cause (verified on the emulator, 1-season Showdown, no CLOSE tap)
The Final Winner screen (`js/seasonFinalV10.js` `finalFrame()` / `seasonSource()`) shows the final season cell ("FINAL SEASON n", both scores) and the trophies only when it has a verified Shared History projection (`history.phase==="HISTORY_CONVERGED"`, then `closedHistoryBound()` after close). Terminal Close forgets the session, and the live Shared History view (`js/productionSharedHistoryConvergence.js`) then clears. JOB-1038 keeps the last converged history in `seasonSource()` (`retainedHistory`), and the automatic close waits up to 15 s for this device's history. But when the **rival's** phone closes first, this phone may never have converged its history, so it shows "FINAL SEASON" with no scores and "Trophy attribution is unavailable right now." Probe on Daniel's page: `{"frame":{"status":"partial","state":"completed"},"hist":null,"term":{"phase":"CLOSED"}}`.

## The change
- `js/seasonFinalV10.js`: when the Showdown is CLOSED for this rivalry and there is no live or retained converged history, read the closed Showdown once with the existing completed-only reader (`root.CareerModeSparkCompletedShowdownReader`, `js/sparkCompletedShowdownReader.js`; it already builds a verified `projection` for a closed Showdown, see how `js/sharedClosedShowdownAdapter.js` uses it). Pass it to `finalFrame()` in the same shape as a converged history: `{authoritative:true, phase:"HISTORY_CONVERGED", rivalryId, projection}`. `closedHistoryBound()` must still verify it; do not loosen that check. Read at most once per closed rivalry per page (cache the promise), then call `wake()` so the screen redraws.
- If the read fails, keep today's partial frame. Never block or hide the Final Winner screen.
- No change to scoring, Rules, the close itself or the history module.
