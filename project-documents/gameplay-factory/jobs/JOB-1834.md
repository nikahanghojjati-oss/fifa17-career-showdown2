## Why
PR #841 (JOB-1808) fixes Standings THIS SHOWDOWN stuck on "Loading standings." mid-Showdown by falling back to the projection
progression already read and checked (`js/sharedActiveShowdownAdapter.js` lines 39-47). The Standings part is right. But
`inspect()` feeds every view: once the fallback sets `p`, classification becomes "active" and `career()` (about line 145) puts the
in-progress projection into `careerInput`. `js/rivalryLegacyV10.js:222` passes `careerInput` to the closed-career loader, so
mid-Showdown Career Statistics shows a record instead of "NO COMPLETED RECORD YET", Trophy Room shows season trophies as won, the
Standings CAREER tab shows in-progress totals, and Legacy still says "No completed showdowns yet". 8 CE2PG checks that pass on r75
now fail: S1/S2.excursion.stats and S1/S2.excursion.trophy for daniel and nik. Full report: `/mnt/project-files/gameplay/r76/verify/PR-841.md`.
