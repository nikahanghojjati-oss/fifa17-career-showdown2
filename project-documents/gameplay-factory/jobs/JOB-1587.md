## What happens now (seen on the emulator, 10 Oct: 1 run in 10)
Both managers publish their season-2 results at the same moment. On one phone the publish first re-reads the finished Transfer Challenge, that read briefly fails, and the publish stops with "The shared Transfer Challenge could not be read." That manager's result is not published until they tap again.

## Why it is safe to continue
A Transfer Challenge that this phone has already seen as COMPLETED for this season can never change again. So when the re-read fails but the last good read already says COMPLETED for the same season, the publish can go on with that.

## The fix (one file)
In `js/productionSharedSeasonResults.js`, function `pssrProviderContext`, the line `await transferApi.refresh();if(!pssrContextMatches(request))pssrFail("SEASON_RESULTS_CONTEXT_STALE");`:
1. Wrap only `await transferApi.refresh()` in `try{...}catch(error){...}`.
2. In the catch: if `pssrContextMatches(request)` and `pssrTransferComplete(request)` are both true, continue (do not throw). Otherwise rethrow `error` unchanged.
3. Keep the `pssrContextMatches` check after it exactly as it is.
- DEFAULT: change nothing else. `setupApi.refresh()` stays as it is. No text, tap order or scoring change.
