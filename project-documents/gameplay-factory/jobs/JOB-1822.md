## What to do (one slice per turn)
1. Take the first slice above that does NOT yet have `project-documents/gameplay-factory/status/HUNT-<slice>.md` on branch
   `factory/gameplay-v1` (for example `HUNT-A01.md`).
2. Open the file on branch **`gameplay/bug-list-1`** and read those lines carefully. You may open at most 3 other files to
   follow a function it calls.
3. Look for these kinds of mistakes, and only these:
   - a season, win, trophy, goal or point counted twice, or not at all (for example after a reload, a rejoin, or when both
     players save the same season);
   - the two managers mixed up (player one's numbers shown or saved as player two's);
   - season numbers off by one (season 0 vs season 1, "Season 3 of 3" vs "Season 4 of 3");
   - the wrong rule for a tie: a tied season is decided by league position; a tied Showdown total shows DRAW;
   - an abandoned or deleted Showdown counted in totals where finished ones should be, or the other way round;
   - a number built from memory only, so it is lost or different after a reload;
   - two screens computing the same number in two different ways, so they can disagree;
   - division by zero, NaN, "undefined" or an empty cell shown to the player;
   - a list sorted or ranked in the wrong order (records, ranks, best season).
   Ignore looks (colours, sizes, layout, pictures, fonts): that is release r77.
4. Write **only real findings**. Each needs: the exact lines (quote 1-5 lines with their line numbers), what the player would
   see, and a concrete example (for example "3-season Showdown, Nik wins seasons 1 and 3: the Trophy Room shows 1 title").
   Give each a confidence: `HIGH` (you can point to the exact wrong line) or `MEDIUM` (likely, but depends on something you
   could not read). Never pad: a slice with no real mistake is a good result, write `No finding`. At most 3 findings per slice.
5. Skip these, they are known and already being fixed: Season 1 of N chip on Home and the header; BACK TO SHOWDOWN HOME landing
   on the dashboard; REVIEW SEASON hidden on the Champion screen; Standings THIS SHOWDOWN stuck loading; Rivalry Statistics button
   hidden or stuck loading; phone Trophy Room ranks and CAREER DETAILS empty; Final Winner cells empty after the close; the
   free-limit message on Home; the two online-record saves refused at the close and at setup; the score compare at
   `js/sparkSharedSeasonCommit.js:81`; the expired session check at `js/sparkSharedSeasonResults.js:63`.
