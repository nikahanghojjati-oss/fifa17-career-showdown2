## Why
JOB-1826 (PR #852) made waiting almost free: while a manager waits on Home, Standings or Season Results with a healthy live
listener on the current season's Results or Commit document, the old 15 s background refreshes are skipped. The GPT review
(`project-documents/gameplay-factory/status/REVIEW-PR-852.md` on `factory/gameplay-v1`, check F) raised a real question:
`psscWaitHealthy()` in `js/productionSharedSeasonCommit.js` also silences refresh owners that read OTHER documents
(`pmspRefreshNow` in `js/productionSharedMultiSeasonProgression.js`: setup, session, rivalry, season commits;
`pfrRefreshNow` in `js/productionSharedFinalReconciliation.js`; `pjrRefreshNow` in `js/productionSharedJourneyReconnect.js`;
`ptcRefreshNow` in `js/productionSharedTerminalClose.js`). If the partner changes one of those documents but not the watched
one, the waiting manager's screen could stay stale. Nik's rule: cutting reads must never slow or break flow, continuity,
consistency, or saving and showing Showdown results on every screen.
