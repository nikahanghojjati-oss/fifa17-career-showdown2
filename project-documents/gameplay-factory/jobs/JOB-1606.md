## What happens now (as the audit saw it)
A Season Result was published and saved, but if the refresh that follows (a brief network drop is enough) fails, the player sees a publish failure and the entry form comes back. Audit finding 1 (line ~140).

## Step 1: VERIFY FIRST (do this before changing anything)
Open `js/productionSharedSeasonResults.js`, the publish function (the one that ends with `view={...result,setup:ctx.setup.setup,rivalryId:ctx.setup.rivalryId};contextKey=request.key;draft=null;await pssrRefreshNow(request);return true;` inside a `try` whose `catch` calls `pssrSetError(error.message||error.code||"Shared Season Result publication failed.")`). The `view=` line above it already shows the waiting state; the read error then surfaces through the read path. Do not touch the write, the `pssrContextMatches` checks or `pssrProviderContext` (job 1587 already changed that function).
