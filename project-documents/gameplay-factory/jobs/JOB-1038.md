## Root cause (verified on the emulator)
A Showdown only counts in Legacy, Trophy Room and Career Statistics after **Terminal Close**: `js/sparkTerminalClose.js` writes `terminalClose` and `terminalProgress` and closes the rivalry. Terminal Close runs only when someone taps CLOSE SHARED SHOWDOWN (`js/productionSharedTerminalClose.js` ~93), and only with an active private session (otherwise BLOCKED, ~142). The automatic final winner screen (BH-8, `js/seasonFinalV10.js` `renderFinal()`) shows CLOSE but never closes. Players who go back to Home without the tap get a Showdown that never counts.

## The change
- `js/productionSharedTerminalClose.js`: when Terminal Close becomes READY on the device that is allowed to close (keep the existing role rule), start the same Terminal Close automatically, exactly once per Showdown and device, using the existing witness, idempotency and retry. If it is BLOCKED (no active session), start it automatically as soon as it becomes READY again (session restored). The other device keeps converging as it does today. Keep the CLOSE button as a manual retry only while an automatic close has failed.
- `js/seasonFinalV10.js`: the final winner screen still shows (never skip, hide or reorder it). While the close runs, show "Saving this Showdown to your career…", and after success show the existing closed state. Use plain words.
- No change to scoring, the final winner rule (DRAW on a tied total, league position for a tied season), or Firestore Rules.
