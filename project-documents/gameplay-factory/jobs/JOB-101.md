## The bug (Nik, 2026-10-05, after a real game, desktop Home)

The six small Home tiles show their icons partly outside the tile, so they are cut off:
- **Start a Showdown** (tactics board): make it bigger and move it slightly left. It must never touch, cover or sit under the "START A SHOWDOWN" text.
- **Legacy**: slightly left and larger if it fits. It may touch the tile's right edge but never leave the tile.
- **Statistics**: part of it touches or goes past the edge. Move it left more than the others, and up a bit; it can be bigger.
- **Trophy Room**: trophy a bit larger.
- **Rule Book**: a little larger, and moved up and left, because part of it is not showing.
- **Settings**: moved up and left, because part of it is hidden (like Statistics).

Rule for every icon, at every desktop width: **fully inside its tile, never touching, covering or hidden by the tile's text.** A clean look matters more than size.

## Why it happens (read on main 61489dd)

- `visual-assets/v10_1/home/home.css:259-261` and `:318-320`: every small-tile icon is `right:-17px; bottom:-20px; width:116px; height:116px`, so it hangs past the tile's right and bottom edges and the tile clips it.
- `css/homeV10.css:80`: Trophy Room is `right:-12px; bottom:-24px; width:108px; height:128px`.
- Desktop sizes: `css/homeV10.css:83-122` (tile height `--tile-h: clamp(136px, 17.5vh, 158px)`, one row of seven tiles; labels drop to 15px at 761-1100px wide and 13px at 761-1000px).
- Tile ids: `#newShowdown`, `#legacyButton`, `#careerStatisticsButton`, `#homeTrophyRoomButton`, `#ruleBookButton`, `#settingsButton`; the icon is `.tileArt` inside each.
