## What this means
1. **The colour is wrong, not only the finish.** Our Matte is a brown espresso. Nik's reference is a neutral graphite black, with almost no colour cast.
2. **The range is too narrow.** Ours sits in a thin dark-brown band, so it reads flat. The reference runs from true black in the recesses, through graphite, to a soft broad pale sheen where the form turns toward the light. That range is what makes it look expensive.
3. **The sheen is soft and broad, never a sharp glint.** Light rolls across the curves like velvet. There are no mirror reflections and no hot spots, which is what separates it from Metallic Black's polished gloss and sharp gold.
4. **Accents are satin aluminium or titanium, not brass.** Gold and brass belong to Metallic Black. Cool satin metal keeps Matte distinct and matches Nik's note.
5. **Warm lamps stay.** The managers' light is fixed. Neutral graphite under the same warm lamps reads as the reference garage does: neutral surfaces lit warm.

## Reference 2 (Nik 01:10 UTC: "it should really feel like premium design and colouring scheme")
A satin black car in soft daylight. It is darker and slightly cooler than reference 1: the body is near-black #0e0e10, the soft sheen on the curves is cool graphite #42474f, and there are no sharp highlights. Both references agree: **neutral to slightly cool graphite black, never brown.** The surfaces are velvet or satin, with form shown by broad soft gradients. The only colour is one small, deliberate accent (red brake calipers in ref 2), which fits Nik's note that "small saturated accents read clean on matte black".

## Target palette for Premium Matte Black (Team V prompts and B7 checks)
- Deepest recesses: #000001-#050606 (true black)
- Main surfaces: #0e0e10-#292928 (neutral graphite black)
- Lit walls behind the men: #22252b-#403e3c (graphite, clearly lighter than the black suits)
- Soft sheen where the form turns to the light: #42474f-#878079 (broad and soft, never a sharp glint)
- Metal accents: satin titanium or brushed aluminium (cool, low sheen), small and few
- Colour cast: saturation under about 0.12 on the room; no brown or espresso tint (blue minus red at least -5)
