## Rules for all 10 (house line from Nik on 2026-10-27, unchanged; same as the pilot)
- Make our own fresh drawing of public heraldry and the real OUTLINE only. Never trace or copy the club's artwork.
- NEVER: club names, letters, initials, monograms, years, mottos, city names, maker or sponsor logos, company marks, or stars that stand for titles (unless they are public heraldry).
- Look: match the pilot exactly, an embroidered cloth patch. That means an embroidered rim in the edge colour, one stitch line inside it, and flat thread-coloured fields with a satin-stitch texture. No gloss, no bevel, no 3D, no drop shadow, no "AI" sheen.
- At 28 px (lists and boards) each crest must still read as the same club. At 256 px (Club Assignment) it should look hand-made.
- Deliver as in HO-084:
  - A transparent PNG 1024 px tall on a 5:6 canvas, with the crest centred and a ~4% margin.
  - 256 px and 64 px exports.
  - SHA256.json.
  - Name each file CREST_IMG_<CLUB>_V1_<size>.png, using the stems in the headings below.
- Run each prompt in a new chat with no club name in it, as in HO-084.

## 1. Manchester United (stem MANUTD)
- Theme: the ship from Manchester's civic arms (public). Below it, a small devil holding a trident, as our own playful drawing. Never the club's devil artwork.
- Outline: a roundel with a black ring band. Inside the ring sits a small heater shield in the lower half, and the ship sits above that shield.
- Ship: a three-masted galleon with full sails in gold, sailing to the viewer's right on two small waves.
- Shield: a red field with the devil in gold, standing and facing the viewer's left, its trident upright.
- Ring band: black with a gold edge, plus 10 small gold stars in place of the lettering (decoration only).
- Colours: red #DA291C, black #171B1F, gold #F4C542. Rim: gold.
- Never: the club name in the ring, the club's own devil artwork, scrolls or footballs added on top.

## 2. Liverpool (stem LIVERPOOL)
- Theme: the liver bird of Liverpool's civic arms (public). It is a cormorant-like bird standing in profile facing the viewer's left, with a sprig of seaweed (laver) in its beak.
- Outline: heater shield. The two side flames (memorial) and the gates with a motto are out. Instead, a plain arched gate band in green across the top may stay, with no words.
- Field: white. The bird is red, with long legs, one foot slightly raised, a crest of three feathers on its head, and wings folded.
- Colours: red #C8102E, white #F2F2F0, gate green #19A48B. Rim: red with gold stitching.
- Never: "You'll Never Walk Alone", the flames, the year, letters.

## 3. Tottenham Hotspur (stem SPURS)
- Theme: a fighting cockerel with spurs standing on an old leather football (a common heraldic bird, our own drawing).
- Outline: a tall roundel or upright oval, with a navy bird on a white field.
- Cockerel: in profile facing the viewer's right, with a proud chest, a high sickle tail of 5-6 long feathers, a comb and wattle, and one foot on the ball. The spurs are visible.
- Ball: a laced 1900s leather ball in navy with white lacing.
- Colours: navy #132257, white #F7F8F8. Rim: navy.
- Never: letters, the club's 2006 bird artwork traced.

## 4. Roma (stem ROMA)
- Theme: the Capitoline she-wolf suckling the twins Romulus and Remus (an ancient public statue).
- Outline: heater shield, split across the top third. The top band is orange-gold and the larger lower field is crimson.
- She-wolf: standing in profile facing the viewer's left, gaunt, head turned slightly toward the viewer, the twins small beneath her. Draw her in crimson on the gold band if she fits; otherwise put her in gold on the crimson field.
- Colours: crimson #8E1D34, orange #E79B28, gold #F2D246. Rim: gold.
- Never: "ASR", "1927", letters, Roman numerals.

## 5. Lyon (stem LYON)
- Theme: the lion of Lyon's civic arms (public), a silver or white lion rampant on red.
- Outline: an arched shield (rounded top and pointed base), with a white field above and a blue base band.
- Lion: rampant, facing the viewer's left, in red on white. Give it a full mane, an open mouth with the tongue showing, the tail curling up in an S, and claws out. Make it big, filling the upper two-thirds.
- Colours: red #D61E35, blue #1F5EA7, white #F5F5F4. Rim: blue.
- Never: the "OL" monogram or any letters.

## 6. Bayer Leverkusen (stem LEVERKUSEN)
- Theme: the Bergisch lion (the public arms of the Berg region), rampant and double-tailed.
- Outline: heater shield, split horizontally, black above and red below.
- Top: a plain white disc centred on the black, left empty. The company cross must not appear.
- Bottom: the lion rampant in white or silver on red, facing the viewer's left, with a crowned head and a double tail.
- Colours: red #D41E31, black #171B1F, white #F5F5F4. Rim: black.
- Never: the company cross, the word Bayer, letters.

## 7. Lazio (stem LAZIO)
- Theme: the eagle of Rome's legions (public), with wings spread and the head turned to the viewer's left.
- Outline: heater shield, sky-blue field with a white border band.
- Eagle: gold and displayed, wings fully spread with long separated primaries, talons gripping a plain bar. It fills the shield.
- Colours: sky blue #77C4E5, white #F5F6F7, gold #D6B735. Rim: white with gold stitching.
- Never: "S.S. Lazio", "1900", letters.

## 8. Eintracht Frankfurt (stem FRANKFURT)
- Theme: the heraldic eagle of Frankfurt's arms (public), black and displayed.
- Outline: roundel. A white disc sits inside a red ring band, and the ring carries 20 small white dots in place of the lettering.
- Eagle: black, displayed, filling the disc, head turned to the viewer's left, with a red beak and tongue and red claws. Draw the wing feathers as clear separate primaries.
- Colours: red #D71F31, black #171B1E, white #F5F5F4. Rim: red.
- Never: letters in the ring.

## 9. Genoa (stem GENOA)
- Theme: the griffin of Genoa (public heraldry), with an eagle head and wings and a lion body, rampant.
- Outline: heater shield, split vertically, red on the viewer's left and blue on the right. An optional small red St George's cross on white sits in a top corner (public, Genoa's flag).
- Griffin: gold, rampant, facing the viewer's left, wings raised, beak open, foreclaws out, lion tail curled.
- Colours: blue #233C73, red #BD1F36, white #F5F5F3, gold #D4AF37. Rim: gold.
- Never: "1893", letters.

## 10. Middlesbrough (stem BORO)
- Theme: a lion rampant (common public heraldry).
- Outline: roundel. A white disc sits inside a red ring band, and the ring carries 20 small white dots in place of the lettering.
- Lion: red rampant lion facing the viewer's left, filling the disc, with a strong mane, open jaw, curled tail and claws.
- Colours: red #D4202F, white #F5F5F5, navy-black #25333E (the ring edge only). Rim: red.
- Never: letters, the year.
