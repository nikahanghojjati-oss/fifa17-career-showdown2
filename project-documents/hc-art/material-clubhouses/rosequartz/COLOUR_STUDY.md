## Nik's direction (27 Nov 2026, his words)
- 04:55 UTC: "This Clubhouse look cute and handcrafted and premium."
- 04:57 UTC: "I want the feeling of marshmallow as well in this."
Read as: soft and charming, never candy. "Marshmallow" is the touch and the shape (plump, pillow-soft cushioned velvet
panels that swell gently off the wall, soft rounded edges, a soft rosy sheen on the curves), not a pastel colour: the
room stays a night room. The word "marshmallow" never goes in a prompt (every word is a magnet).

## The look in words
A private members' club at night in **rose quartz** (dusty rose, milky and softly cloudy, light passing a little way into
the stone, hard natural stone), **plump cushioned plum velvet** wall panels, **plum-black wood** frames, and **satin rose
gold** as small accents only. The stone is the jewel and the palest thing in the room; the velvet sits deeper.

## Siblings (playbook): nearest are Rhodochrosite and Amethyst

- vs Amethyst: pale rose stone vs deep violet crystal; rose gold vs silver; plum with a wine lean vs violet plum.
- vs Ruby: dusty pale rose vs deep wine red veined marble.
