### Lessons from the pilot (built into batch 1)
1. Big plain rose quartz reads as plaster or smoke; hand-cut blocks with fine straight joints plus the "hand-carved rose quartz bowl seen by candlelight" line read as real stone (Board T1/E1 6.5-7.5, T3 9).
2. Puffs must be named for every panel, or tall panels come back flat (Board T2).
3. An edit that names cool hexes drags the velvet to grape even when it says "keep the velvet" (Board E1: 345 to 321).
4. Astra warms rose and plum by 15-35 degrees: velvet #7A4474 lands at hue 337-345, stone #BD7CA6 at 343-350.
5. Home T1 came back with almost no lamp pool; the batch 1 light line now says each lamp's wash reaches across the nearest panels, warms the round tops of the puffs and deepens the stitched seams (the edit that took Home to 9).
6. Words never used: marshmallow, glow, glossy, mirror, polished, damask.
