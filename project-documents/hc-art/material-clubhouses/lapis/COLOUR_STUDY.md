## Nik's direction for Lapis (26 Nov 2026 18:37 UTC, his words)
"I want the lapis to really look vibrant and in some type of way even vivid. It looks like a very, like a lively touch."
So Lapis is the one clubhouse that is **vivid**: rich, saturated, jewel-bright ultramarine with lively gold flecks. The
"a touch muted and aged" line used for Ruby and Emerald is **not** used for Lapis. It is still a room **at night** (the
job file's light rule), and still real stone and leather, never flat plastic blue: vivid colour, not glowing stone.

## The look in words
A private members' club at night built from real blue stone and leather: **polished lapis lazuli** (a vivid lapis-blue
stone with a few soft cloud-like drifts of deeper and brighter blue and small scattered flakes of diamond, clear glass
and gem crystal), **lapis-blue leather** upholstered wall panels, smooth satin-finish, with neat stitched seams, **ebony** frames
so dark they read blue-black (never brown), and **satin gold** used only as small accents (lamp arms, the counter rail,
handles, hairline inlays). Polished lapis may hold soft reflections of the lamps; nothing else shines.

Nik's colour pick (26 Nov 18:40 UTC): he showed four ChatGPT lapis shades (Lapis #26619C, Lapis Vivid #165CC4, Lapis Vivid
Vibrant #1268E8, Lapis Vivid Vibrant Lively #3485FF) and asked which is more premium and fits the clubhouse set. The pick
is Lapis Vivid for walls and stone (rich and saturated, still reads as stone), Vibrant only in the lamp pools; #26619C reads
grey (a blue-tinted Graphite at night) and #3485FF reads electric, like a screen, not stone.

Nik (26 Nov 18:40 UTC, his words): "Instead of gold this time, instead of gold flake, we're gonna use diamond flake and
glass flake and gem flake." So Lapis has NO gold pyrite: the stone carries tiny diamond, clear glass and gem-crystal flakes,
each a small crisp pinprick of white-blue light. Satin gold stays only as small metal trim.
Nik (18:41 UTC): "we want it to look like premium, and we don't want it to look AI generated at all." The flakes are the
biggest AI-look risk: few, tiny, scattered like real stone, never glitter; every picture gets a 2x eye check for AI texture.
Nik (18:42 UTC) asked how to make it the most premium, "and looked handcrafted and not AI generated". The three moves,
in every Lapis prompt from the pilot on: (1) the stone is the jewel: leather walls one step deeper (#1450AE) than the lapis
(#165CC4) so the stone stands out; (2) the stone varies like real stone (soft clouds of deeper and brighter blue, a sparse
scattering of tiny flakes); (3) small satin gold trim, bright only at the lamps. Plus hand-cut slabs, hand-stitched seams,
"like a photograph of a real private club, never a render or an AI image".

## Light rule (written into EVERY Lapis prompt; confirm with Nik on the pilot)
Lapis is vivid, lively ultramarine lapis lazuli and ultramarine leather at night: rich, saturated and jewel-bright, like
a lapis gem held to candlelight, never flat plastic blue, never navy-grey, never purple or teal. Each warm lamp throws a
clearly bright pool where the blue turns a brighter, clearer ultramarine (never grey, green or purple), and the blue
falls off to a deep ultramarine near-black only in the far corners. The stone carries tiny diamond, glass and gem flakes,
each one small crisp pinprick of light, never glitter or starbursts; satin gold is only small trim.

## Where warm lamp light and gold may show
- **Lamp light:** a clearly bright warm pool on the leather or stone right around each existing lamp (Board: the wall
  lamp at the left; Home: the table lamp on the sideboard, the wall lamp on the right pillar, plus the stadium glow
  through the window). Warm light over blue drifts to grey, green or purple, so the prompts say the lit blue becomes a
  brighter, clearer ultramarine.
- **Gold:** lamp arms and bases, the thin counter rail, door and window handles, hairline inlays.
  No gold flecks in the stone (Nik 18:40 UTC). No gold wall, panel or pillar. The rail is a soft dim satin line, never a bright yellow line.
- **Reflections:** counter and pillar lapis are polished and may show one soft reflection of the lamp and the dark suits.
  No mirror floor, no chrome, no glitter.

## Seating (Nik's decision, 25 Nov 2026 07:36 UTC, standing for every material clubhouse)
The Home armchairs and sofa are reupholstered in the clubhouse's own material and colour (Lapis: the same vivid
ultramarine leather as the walls, a touch brighter where lamp light falls so they read against the walls), keeping their
exact shape, size, cushions, seams and place. The plaid blankets, ball, whisky, grey throw, rug and lamp shades keep their
own colours.

## What Lapis must never be
- Flat plastic or poster blue: vivid comes from real stone drifts, crystal flakes and leather, never one flat
  saturated fill (spread and detail catch this; see the flat recolour below).
- Navy-grey, slate or denim: that is Metallic Black or Graphite tinted blue.
- Purple or violet (that is Amethyst's lane, over 255 deg), teal or turquoise (under 200 deg), pale sky blue or pastel.
- Brown: none of Wooden's tan, honey or orange-brown wood may survive. Ebony reads blue-black.
- Glitter or AI sparkle: the diamond, glass and gem flakes are tiny crisp pinpricks, few and scattered, never a field
  of sparkle, starbursts, lens flare or halos. Never gold flecks (Nik 18:40 UTC).
- Busy stone: no veins web, crackle, cracked ice or tight marbling. Lapis is a calm field of blue with a few soft drifts
  and scattered flecks.

## Numeric bar: `tools/material-clubhouses/lapis_check.py` (limits in `material_check.py` LOOKS["LAPIS"])
Measured outside the dilated hero mask at 1536x864, room pixels only for diff, mw and spread. Ruby and Emerald are siblings.
| Check | Limit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| vs silver / black / matte / ruby / emerald | mean diff >= 15 each | unmistakably a new clubhouse |
| mw (vs Wooden) | >= 12 | every big surface changed |
| hue | >= 0.50 of coloured pixels in 200-255 deg | lapis blue (Nik's pick 216 deg); under 200 is teal, over 255 is violet |
| brown | <= 0.20 in Wooden's 15-45 deg band | only the small satin gold trim sits in this band |
| sat | median 0.45-0.97 | **vivid** (Nik 18:37 UTC): no 0.90 ceiling as on Ruby/Emerald; a floor of 0.45 keeps it from going grey |
| pale | <= 0.03 of the room | never pale sky blue or pastel |
| gold | <= 0.04 of the room | satin gold trim only; no gold flecks (Nik 18:40 UTC) |
| luma | 14-70 | night, but room for a jewel-bright blue (Ruby/Emerald 14-60) |
| glint | <= max(3, min(8, 0.6 x Black)) per 10k px | crystal flakes are pinpricks; if they alone trip this on an approved pilot, recalibrate here with the reason |
| spread | >= max(Wooden, 0.75 x Black), room pixels | rich, not flat |
| detail | >= 1.05 x Wooden | readable leather seams, lapis drifts and flecks |

Flat recolour test (26 Nov): a luma-mapped recolour of Wooden Board and Home onto this palette passes every colour test
(hue 0.999, sat 0.79) but MISSES spread (11 vs 36, 34 vs 68) and detail (1.3 vs 2.5, 1.5 vs 3.0). Vivid by fill alone
cannot pass.

These limits are **provisional**: I re-calibrate them on the pilot Nik approves and write any change here with its reason.
Passing the numbers is necessary, never sufficient: the HO-173 eye check and Nik's OK on the board decide.
