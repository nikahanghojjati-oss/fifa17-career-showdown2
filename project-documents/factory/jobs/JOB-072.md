## Steps

Do them in order. After each step update `project-documents/factory/status/JOB-072.md` and push with the message `Job 72 step k/9: <step name>`.

1. Set up `visual-assets/v10_1/legacy/` by copying the structure of `visual-assets/v10_1/home/` (index.html, css, js, tools/build_preview.py) and renaming. Link `../shared/showdown-tokens.css`, `showdown-type.css`, `showdown-ui.css`, `stage.css`, `motion.css` and the shared JS. Read `visual-assets/v10_1/legacy/TRUTH.md` and `fixtures.json` (from the truth job) and render every frame from fixtures.json via `?frame=`.

2. Stage: load `visual-assets/v10_1/legacy/assets/ENV_*_PLATE_V1` through the stage engine. REGISTRATION RULE: at 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 1366 × 768) the plate is drawn exactly where the mockup has it (cover, centred, no extra zoom, no shift), so Daniel, Nik, hands and stadium are the mockup's own pixels in the mockup's own places. Other desktop ratios may crop edges but never scale faces more than ±5 % from the 16:9 position. Atmosphere layer on. Mark the managers' areas with `data-manager="daniel"` (left) and `data-manager="nik"` (right) for the QA harness.

3. Title block: the screen title is the brush wordmark IMAGE (`TITLE_*_V1.webp` from the plate or wordmark job) with the real words in visually-hidden text for screen readers, never a font. Eyebrow and tagline in the shared type classes. Place everything exactly where the mockup has it (measure the mockup: centre x, top y, width as % of the frame). No dark box behind text unless the mockup has one there; the plate is fully clean, so UI floats on the scene. Every illustrated object in the mockup (trophy, icon art, frame) gets a real art asset, not a flat box.

4. Main layout (desktop, ≥ 1024 px): Archive panel spans 4 %–96 % width from about 41 % to 82 % height; side menu 19 % of the panel; cards 4 × 2 with 12 px gaps. VIEW SEASON HISTORY as the one primary action, centred below the panel.

5. Build the mockup elements one by one, following the two lists above ("what to take" and "product truth") and the KEEP / CHANGE / DROP table in TRUTH.md. Measure each element's position and size on the mockup (as % of 1366 × 768) and match it within about 2 %. Every word that can change is DOM text from fixtures.json.

6. Depth sandwich: The managers lean over the panel: their lower chests are behind the panel's top edge. Add a soft shadow on the panel's top edge under each body so they look like they stand behind a desk, not cut in half. If Daniel's hand on chin sits close to the panel edge, keep it fully visible (never cut a hand with the panel). Make the cut-outs with `shared/tools/cutout.py` from the platemap polygons; add rim light and contact shadows.

7. States: build every frame in fixtures.json (LG1 eight completed Showdowns over two pages (preview data) · LG2 one active Showdown plus history · LG3 abandoned Showdown row · LG4 new career empty · LG5 current-Showdown-only label · LG6 unavailable). Preview frames show the "Preview data" chip. Empty, partial and unavailable states are designed panels with an icon and plain words, never blank space.

8. Desktop QA: run `shared/tools/factory-qa.cjs` for all frames at the desktop sizes; run the mockup-diff gate on the main frame at 1920 × 1080 (`python3 visual-assets/v10_1/shared/tools/mockup_diff.py --mockup <mockup> --build <shot> --plate <plate 1X> --platemap <platemap.json> --out visual-assets/v10_1/legacy/evidence/diff/`) and make it PASS (faces ≥ 0.90, other protected boxes ≥ 0.75, SSIM ≥ plate SSIM − 0.15, ΔE ≤ plate ΔE + 6). Check page weight on first paint ≤ 900 KB desktop (WebP, `<picture>`, never ship PNG masters). Iterate until your own scoring against QUALITY_BAR criteria 1–7 and 10 is at least 4 each.

9. Write `visual-assets/v10_1/legacy/BUILD_RESULT.md` (how to run, frames, what you changed from the mockup and why, QA results, known gaps) and `preview.html` with build_preview.py. Commit and finish.
