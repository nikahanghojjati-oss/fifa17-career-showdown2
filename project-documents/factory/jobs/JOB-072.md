2. Stage: load `visual-assets/v10_1/legacy/assets/ENV_*_PLATE_V1` through the stage engine with the platemap focal point. Atmosphere layer on. Mark the managers' areas with `data-manager="daniel"` (left) and `data-manager="nik"` (right) for the QA harness.

3. Title block: eyebrow (crown + CAREER MODE SHOWDOWN 17), brush title and tagline with the shared type classes, placed exactly where the mockup has them (measure the mockup: centre x, top y, width as % of viewport).

4. Main layout (desktop, ≥ 1024 px): Archive panel spans 4 %–96 % width from about 41 % to 82 % height; side menu 19 % of the panel; cards 4 × 2 with 12 px gaps. VIEW SEASON HISTORY as the one primary action, centred below the panel.

5. Build the mockup elements one by one, following the two lists above ("what to take" and "product truth") and the KEEP / CHANGE / DROP table in TRUTH.md. Measure each element's position and size on the mockup (as % of 1366 × 768) and match it within about 2 %. Every word that can change is DOM text from fixtures.json.

6. Depth sandwich: The managers lean over the panel: their lower chests are behind the panel's top edge. Add a soft shadow on the panel's top edge under each body so they look like they stand behind a desk, not cut in half. Make the cut-outs with `shared/tools/cutout.py` from the platemap polygons; add rim light and contact shadows.

7.
