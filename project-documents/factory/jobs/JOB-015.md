## Steps

Do them in order. After each step update `project-documents/factory/status/JOB-015.md` and push with the message `Job 15 step k/6: <step name>`.

1. Study how Home and League place the plate and overlays (object-fit, focal points, scaling). Write the rules into `visual-assets/v10_1/shared/STAGE.md`.

2. Write `visual-assets/v10_1/shared/stage.css` and `visual-assets/v10_1/shared/stage.js`: a `.sd-stage` container with layers 0–4 (plate, atmosphere, ui, cutout, light). The plate and the cut-outs share one inner box that is scaled with `object-fit: cover` math in JS from the plate's size and a focal point; the UI layer is a normal responsive layout on top. Serve `_2X` files for DPR ≥ 2 (`image-set`).

3. Phone mode (portrait ≤ 760 px wide): the stage switches to the face band (focal crop from platemap `phone_band`), with the content below. The cut-out layer follows the same crop.

4. Atmosphere layer: slow drifting dust (CSS, ≤ 30 particles), light flare sweep, vignette. All off with reduced motion.

5. Light layer: rim-light masks tinted gold (from cutout.py `--rim`), contact shadow helper class, 2 % film grain.

6. Demo: `visual-assets/v10_1/shared/stage-demo.html` with the League plate and Daniel's finger overlay re-done through the engine, plus a fake panel overlapping the hand to prove the sandwich. Screenshot at 1366 × 768, 1920 × 1080, 1366 × 640, 393 × 660 and 360 × 640; check the overlay sits within 1 device pixel of the plate at every size. Commit and finish.
