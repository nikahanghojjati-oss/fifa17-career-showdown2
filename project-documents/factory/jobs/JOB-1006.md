## The bug (Nik, 6 Oct)

In a shared Showdown, after the **last** season is committed, the game jumps straight to the Final Winner screen, so neither manager ever sees that last season's score. Nik chose: **no extra tap, one screen** shows the last season's score **and** the Final Winner, and the winner effects stay.

## The design (decided by the lead; do exactly this)

The Final Winner results panel already has a summary strip with two cells, SEASONS and MARGIN. Add a **third cell, first in the strip**, for the final season:

```
FINAL SEASON 3          <- label (small, like "SEASONS")
7 – 2                   <- Daniel's season points, dash, Nik's season points (Daniel always LEFT)
DANIEL TAKES THE SEASON <- result line (small, like "PLAYED")
```

- **Desktop (wider than 760 px):** the strip shows three cells in a row: FINAL SEASON, SEASONS, MARGIN. The final-season cell is a little wider (1.4 parts vs 1 and 1). The whole left part of the panel grows from 19% to 31% so the honours table keeps the rest.
- **Phone (760 px and narrower):** the SUMMARY tab is visible first and has lots of empty room. The final-season cell goes on its own full-width row on top (bigger score), SEASONS and MARGIN stay side by side under it. No new tab, nothing to tap.
- Nothing moves in the title, the winner heading, the totals or the trophy, so the winner effects are not covered.
