## Steps

Do them in order. After each step update `project-documents/factory/status/JOB-095.md` and push with the message `Job 95 step k/10: <step name>`.

1. Set up `visual-assets/v10_1/settings/` by copying the structure of `visual-assets/v10_1/home/` (index.html, css, js, tools/build_preview.py) and renaming. Link `../shared/showdown-tokens.css`, `showdown-type.css`, `showdown-ui.css`, `stage.css`, `motion.css` and the shared JS. Read `visual-assets/v10_1/settings/TRUTH.md` and `fixtures.json` (from the truth job) and render every frame from fixtures.json via `?frame=`.

2. Stage: the system plate `visual-assets/v10_1/shared/plates/ENV_SYS_PLATE_V1` through the stage engine with a heavier dark scrim (text panels need calm behind them). No managers on this screen.

3. Title block: eyebrow (crown + CAREER MODE SHOWDOWN 17), brush title and tagline with the shared type classes, placed exactly where the mockup has them (measure the mockup: centre x, top y, width as % of viewport).

4. Main layout (desktop, ≥ 1024 px): No own mockup: style it from the system. System plate with a heavier scrim. Brush title "SETTINGS". Panels exactly as settings.js builds them (eyebrow, title, description): motion preference choice (two large radio cards), menu feedback toggle, app version and asset revision info rows, any account rows. Toggles and radio cards are gold-on-black, 44 px targets, visible focus rings, aria states unchanged from the product. Any destructive action sits at the bottom in a "Danger zone" panel with a red-edged button and a confirm dialog. Phone: one column, panel content scrolls inside itself if needed; Back pinned.

5. Build the mockup elements one by one, following the two lists above ("what to take" and "product truth") and the KEEP / CHANGE / DROP table in TRUTH.md. Measure each element's position and size on the mockup (as % of 1366 × 768) and match it within about 2 %. Every word that can change is DOM text from fixtures.json.

6. Depth sandwich: No characters on this screen. Make the cut-outs with `shared/tools/cutout.py` from the platemap polygons; add rim light and contact shadows.

7. States: build every frame in fixtures.json (ST1 default · ST2 reduced motion on · ST3 a confirm dialog open (if a destructive action exists)). Preview frames show the "Preview data" chip. Empty, partial and unavailable states are designed panels with an icon and plain words, never blank space.

8. Desktop QA: run `shared/tools/factory-qa.cjs` for all frames at the desktop sizes; make the compare sheet against the mockup at 1366 × 768 (`visual-assets/v10_1/settings/evidence/COMPARE_1366.png`). Iterate until your own scoring against QUALITY_BAR criteria 1–7 and 10 is at least 4 each.

9. Phone in the same job: Any destructive action sits at the bottom in a "Danger zone" panel with a red-edged button and a confirm dialog. Phone: one column, panel content scrolls inside itself if needed; Back pinned. Measure 393 × 660, 360 × 640 and 375 × 553 with factory-qa (the page never scrolls; a content panel may scroll inside itself).

10. Write `visual-assets/v10_1/settings/BUILD_RESULT.md` (how to run, frames, what you changed from the mockup and why, QA results, known gaps) and `preview.html` with build_preview.py. Commit and finish.
