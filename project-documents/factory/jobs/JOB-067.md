## The mockup and what to take from it

- Mockup: project-documents/factory/mockups/MOCKUP_RIVALRY_STATISTICS.png
- Centre: one tall comparison panel. Header row: Daniel's crest, "DANIEL" and club name on the left; Nik's on the right. Then seven rows: big number left, gold icon, label, big number right.
- Bottom: three panels in a row: HEAD-TO-HEAD (Daniel wins / Nik wins / Draws as giant numerals), SEASON-BY-SEASON (season, Daniel score, Nik score, winner), TROPHY CABINET (four trophies).
- Daniel left, pointing at the viewer/panel with his index finger: the most energetic pose in the set. Nik right, arms crossed.
- One button: BACK TO SHOWDOWN HOME.

## Product truth that overrides the mockup

- Crests are our original crests for each manager's current club (getClubCrestSvg). Club names as text are fine.
- Rows only from recorded data. TRANSFER SIGNINGS only when transfer history is available; otherwise the row says "Unavailable" (never 0).
- Season winner icon: the winner's crest; a draw shows a gold dash and "Draw" (tie-break rules from PRODUCT_TRUTH §2 already decide most ties).
- Trophy cabinet uses our trophies (jobs 19–22) with per-manager counts.

## Steps

Do them in order. After each step update `project-documents/factory/status/JOB-067.md` and push with the message `Job 67 step k/9: <step name>`.

1. Set up `visual-assets/v10_1/rivalry-statistics/` by copying the structure of `visual-assets/v10_1/home/` (index.html, css, js, tools/build_preview.py) and renaming. Link `../shared/showdown-tokens.css`, `showdown-type.css`, `showdown-ui.css`, `stage.css`, `motion.css` and the shared JS. Read `visual-assets/v10_1/rivalry-statistics/TRUTH.md` and `fixtures.json` (from the truth job) and render every frame from fixtures.json via `?frame=`.

2. Stage: load `visual-assets/v10_1/rivalry-statistics/assets/ENV_*_PLATE_V1` through the stage engine. REGISTRATION RULE: at 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 1366 × 768) the plate is drawn exactly where the mockup has it (cover, centred, no extra zoom, no shift), so Daniel, Nik, hands and stadium are the mockup's own pixels in the mockup's own places. Other desktop ratios may crop edges but never scale faces more than ±5 % from the 16:9 position. Atmosphere layer on. Mark the managers' areas with `data-manager="daniel"` (left) and `data-manager="nik"` (right) for the QA harness.

3. Title block: the screen title is the brush wordmark IMAGE (`TITLE_*_V1.webp` from the plate or wordmark job) with the real words in visually-hidden text for screen readers, never a font. Eyebrow and tagline in the shared type classes. Place everything exactly where the mockup has it (measure the mockup: centre x, top y, width as % of the frame). No dark box behind text unless the mockup has one there; the plate is fully clean, so UI floats on the scene. Every illustrated object in the mockup (trophy, icon art, frame) gets a real art asset, not a flat box.

4. Main layout (desktop, ≥ 1024 px): Comparison panel centred, about 44 % width, from just under the tagline to about 63 % height. Leader's number in each row in gold, the other in white. Three bottom panels share one baseline, equal height, with the button centred below.

5. Build the mockup elements one by one, following the two lists above ("what to take" and "product truth") and the KEEP / CHANGE / DROP table in TRUTH.md. Measure each element's position and size on the mockup (as % of 1366 × 768) and match it within about 2 %. Every word that can change is DOM text from fixtures.json.

6. Depth sandwich: Daniel's pointing hand reaches toward the comparison panel's left edge: cut out his hand and forearm and lay them OVER the panel edge, with a 3 px contact shadow on the panel. This is the screen's signature "out of the menu" moment; it must look real. Nik's crossed arms near the right edge of the panel: if they overlap, same treatment. Make the cut-outs with `shared/tools/cutout.py` from the platemap polygons; add rim light and contact shadows.

7. States: build every frame in fixtures.json (RV1 active Showdown mid-way (preview data) · RV2 completed Showdown · RV3 first season, nothing played yet · RV4 transfer data unavailable). Preview frames show the "Preview data" chip. Empty, partial and unavailable states are designed panels with an icon and plain words, never blank space.

8. Desktop QA: run `shared/tools/factory-qa.cjs` for all frames at the desktop sizes; run the mockup-diff gate on the main frame at 1920 × 1080 (`python3 visual-assets/v10_1/shared/tools/mockup_diff.py --mockup <mockup> --build <shot> --plate <plate 1X> --platemap <platemap.json> --out visual-assets/v10_1/rivalry-statistics/evidence/diff/`) and make it PASS (faces ≥ 0.90, other protected boxes ≥ 0.75, SSIM ≥ plate SSIM − 0.15, ΔE ≤ plate ΔE + 6). Check page weight on first paint ≤ 900 KB desktop (WebP, `<picture>`, never ship PNG masters). Iterate until your own scoring against QUALITY_BAR criteria 1–7 and 10 is at least 4 each.

9. Write `visual-assets/v10_1/rivalry-statistics/BUILD_RESULT.md` (how to run, frames, what you changed from the mockup and why, QA results, known gaps) and `preview.html` with build_preview.py. Commit and finish.
