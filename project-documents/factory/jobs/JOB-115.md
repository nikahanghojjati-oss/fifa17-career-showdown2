## Steps

Do them in order. After each step update `project-documents/factory/status/JOB-115.md` and push with the message `Job 115 step k/6: <step name>`.

1. Cut-outs: with `visual-assets/v10_1/shared/tools/cutout.py`, draw a polygon around Daniel's whole visible figure (head, hair, shoulders, arms, hands, down to the plate's bottom edge or where his body leaves the frame) and one around Nik. Hair keeps a soft edge. Save `visual-assets/v10_1/trophy-room/assets/OVL_TR_DANIEL_PHONE_V1.webp` and `OVL_TR_NIK_PHONE_V1.webp` (transparent, about 1000 px tall, quality 85) plus PNG masters, and the polygons in `visual-assets/v10_1/trophy-room/assets/phonemap.json`.

2. Portrait guide: from the plate, take the centre region at 9:16 (full height), fill every part of Daniel and Nik inside it solid cyan (#00FFFF) with an 8 px margin, and save `visual-assets/v10_1/trophy-room/assets/GUIDE_TR_PHONE.png`.

3. Image edit (one request) on the guide with exactly this prompt:

```
Edit this image. Repaint every solid cyan area as empty night stadium continuing what is around it: crowd bokeh, floodlights, banners, dark atmosphere and warm golden light, same perspective. There are no people in the final image. Keep everything that is not cyan as it is. Add no text, no letters, no logos, no people. Portrait 9:16, the largest size available.
```
Check: no cyan, no people, no text or logos, light matches. Retry in a NEW chat up to 3 times, always from the guide.

4. Export the background as `visual-assets/v10_1/trophy-room/assets/ENV_TR_PHONE_V1.webp` at 1179 × 2096 (quality 80, aim ≤ 250 KB) plus a PNG master.

5. Proof: composite a 393 × 660 (×3) frame in Python: background cover, Daniel and Nik cut-outs large in the top 55 % (heads fully visible, Daniel left, both slightly overlapping the centre and the area below), and a dark gradient at the bottom where the buttons will sit. Save `visual-assets/v10_1/trophy-room/assets/PHONE_PROOF.png` and write the cut-out positions (as % of the frame) into phonemap.json.

6. Weight: phone art total (background + two cut-outs) ≤ 350 KB so the screen's first paint stays ≤ 450 KB. Write sizes and SHA-256 into `visual-assets/v10_1/trophy-room/assets/phone_intake.md`. Commit and finish.
