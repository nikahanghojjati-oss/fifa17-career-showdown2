## Steps

Do them in order. After each step update `project-documents/factory/status/JOB-031.md` and push with the message `Job 31 step k/5: <step name>`.

1. Render HM1–HM3 at 1366 × 768 and 1920 × 1080 with `&mends=0` and with mends on; crop both faces, Daniel's pointing hand, and every seam-mend box at 400 %. Save to `visual-assets/v10_1/home/evidence/edges_before/`.

2. List every defect (soft tone patch, seam line, halo, colour fringe) with its pixel box in `visual-assets/v10_1/home/evidence/edges_before/DEFECTS.md`.

3. Fix each defect at the source: redo the seam mend with a better-matched patch (sample tone from the 12 px ring around it, match mean and variance), or re-cut the overlay with `shared/tools/cutout.py`. Never paint over a face feature.

4. Re-render the same crops into `edges_after/` and make a before/after sheet `visual-assets/v10_1/home/evidence/EDGES_BEFORE_AFTER.png`.

5. Re-run Home's render-qa; all previous gates still pass. Update BUILD_RESULT.md (Edges section). Commit and finish.
