## The mockup and what to take from it

- Mockup: project-documents/factory/mockups/MOCKUP_SEASON_RESULTS.jpg and MOCKUP_TROPHY_ROOM.png
- This screen is the Showdown's final whistle: the biggest moment in the app. Use the Trophy Room plate and the Showdown Champion trophy (job 19) as the centrepiece.
- Title: "SHOWDOWN CHAMPION" in the brush style; under it the winner's name huge (e.g. "DANIEL"), then the final totals "28 – 24" (Daniel left).
- A results strip: seasons played, season wins each, trophies each (all from recorded data).
- Buttons: the product's real actions (TRUTH.md; e.g. VIEW SEASON HISTORY, BACK TO HOME).

## Product truth that overrides the mockup

- The final winner is decided by total Showdown points only; equal totals = "SHOWDOWN DRAWN" (both sides lit equally, no crown).
- Completion pending (Terminal Close not yet verified): show the result with a visible "Completion pending" chip; do not hide it.
- Winner side lighting: the winner's half of the plate gets a warm spotlight; the other half dims to 70 %, never to black (respect the rival).
- No bottom bar on this screen (it is hidden here, V2G-003): use the full 393 × 660 on phone. On desktop the top bar may sit in the top 52 px; keep that strip free of faces and titles.

## Steps

Do them in order. After each step update `project-documents/factory/status/JOB-082.md` and push with the message `Job 82 step k/9: <step name>`.

1. Set up `visual-assets/v10_1/final-winner/` by copying the structure of `visual-assets/v10_1/home/` (index.html, css, js, tools/build_preview.py) and renaming. Link `../shared/showdown-tokens.css`, `showdown-type.css`, `showdown-ui.css`, `stage.css`, `motion.css` and the shared JS. Read `visual-assets/v10_1/final-winner/TRUTH.md` and `fixtures.json` (from the truth job) and render every frame from fixtures.json via `?frame=`.

2. Stage: load `visual-assets/v10_1/trophy-room/assets/ENV_*_PLATE_V1` through the stage engine. REGISTRATION RULE: at 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 1366 × 768) the plate is drawn exactly where the mockup has it (cover, centred, no extra zoom, no shift), so Daniel, Nik, hands and stadium are the mockup's own pixels in the mockup's own places. Other desktop ratios may crop edges but never scale faces more than ±5 % from the 16:9 position. Atmosphere layer on. Mark the managers' areas with `data-manager="daniel"` (left) and `data-manager="nik"` (right) for the QA harness.

3. Title block: the screen title is the brush wordmark IMAGE (`TITLE_*_V1.webp` from the plate or wordmark job) with the real words in visually-hidden text for screen readers, never a font. Eyebrow and tagline in the shared type classes. Place everything exactly where the mockup has it (measure the mockup: centre x, top y, width as % of the frame). No dark box behind text unless the mockup has one there; the plate is fully clean, so UI floats on the scene. Every illustrated object in the mockup (trophy, icon art, frame) gets a real art asset, not a flat box.

4. Main layout (desktop, ≥ 1024 px): Trophy centred and larger than in Trophy Room (about 42 % of height), confetti layer behind the managers and in front of the stadium. Winner name and totals under the title, results strip and buttons at the bottom.

5. Build the mockup elements one by one, following the two lists above ("what to take" and "product truth") and the KEEP / CHANGE / DROP table in TRUTH.md. Measure each element's position and size on the mockup (as % of 1366 × 768) and match it within about 2 %. Every word that can change is DOM text from fixtures.json.

6. Depth sandwich: The trophy is in front of everything except the managers' nearest arms. Reuse the Trophy Room cut-outs. Make the cut-outs with `shared/tools/cutout.py` from the platemap polygons; add rim light and contact shadows.

7. States: build every frame in fixtures.json (FW1 Daniel wins · FW2 Nik wins · FW3 draw · FW4 result reconciled, completion pending · FW5 completed). Preview frames show the "Preview data" chip. Empty, partial and unavailable states are designed panels with an icon and plain words, never blank space.

8. Desktop QA: run `shared/tools/factory-qa.cjs` for all frames at the desktop sizes; run the mockup-diff gate on the main frame at 1920 × 1080 (`python3 visual-assets/v10_1/shared/tools/mockup_diff.py --mockup <mockup> --build <shot> --plate <plate 1X> --platemap <platemap.json> --out visual-assets/v10_1/final-winner/evidence/diff/`) and make it PASS (faces ≥ 0.90, other protected boxes ≥ 0.75, SSIM ≥ plate SSIM − 0.15, ΔE ≤ plate ΔE + 6). Check page weight on first paint ≤ 900 KB desktop (WebP, `<picture>`, never ship PNG masters). Iterate until your own scoring against QUALITY_BAR criteria 1–7 and 10 is at least 4 each.

9. Write `visual-assets/v10_1/final-winner/BUILD_RESULT.md` (how to run, frames, what you changed from the mockup and why, QA results, known gaps) and `preview.html` with build_preview.py. Commit and finish.
