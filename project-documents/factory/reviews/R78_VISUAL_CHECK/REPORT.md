# r78 visual check (1.9.2-r78, main f15f54f9)

Checked 17 Oct 2026 by Claude for the Team V lead. This is a report only: no code was changed, nothing was pushed to main and no PR was opened.

## How it was checked

- **Build:** a detached worktree of `origin/main` at `f15f54f9` (r78 "look and feel", #897), served locally with `tests/support/static-server.cjs`. The live site cannot be reached from this container (the proxy returns 403), so this is the same build served on 127.0.0.1.
- **Browser:** Chromium 1194 from `/opt/pw-browsers`, through Playwright 1.56.
- **Reaching screens:** the openers come from `tests/browser/phone-marble-facecheck-audit.cjs` on main. They seed a demo Showdown with read-only provider snapshots, and Firebase is never reached. Loading uses the approach of `loading-visual-audit.cjs`. Settings and the confirm box follow `settings-layout-audit.cjs`, `connected-account-settings-audit.cjs` and `support/marble-confirm.cjs`. The error screen is the real sign-in gate (`CareerModeOnlinePlayerIdentity.openGate()`), and the toast is the real `showAppNotice()`.
- **Sizes:** desktop 1440x900 and 1920x1080; phone 393x852, 393x660, 375x667 and 360x640; one sideways phone at 852x393.
- **Signed in:** this is simulated. The identity module's published state is swapped for a ready "Daniel" identity, and its own render path then draws the labels. Real Google sign-in was not used.
- **Flash test:** every compositor frame was recorded through a CDP screencast. This covered a cold load and real nav or tile clicks Home → Rules → Home → Trophy → Home → Legacy → Home → Stats → Home → Settings → close → Standings → Home. It ran at 393x852 and 1440x900 with the device asking for reduced motion, once with the app on FOLLOW DEVICE and once with FULL MOTION. The cold load was also run at 360x640 and 852x393 with 1x and 4x CPU throttle.
- **Face check:** the repo's own `phone-marble-facecheck-audit.cjs` was run against this build. The result is **PASS** on all 12 screens at all 4 phone sizes: no UI covers a face zone, and no manager layer is faded, clipped or filtered.
- **Judging:** every screenshot below was opened and looked at before it was judged. Targets are `NEXT_<SCREEN>.png` (desktop) and `PHONE_<SCREEN>_MARBLE.png` (phone) from `project-documents/factory/mockups/`. They are treated as look targets, not pixel specs.

**How the sheets are laid out:**
- `*_phone.webp`: the phone target, then the live screen at 393x852, 393x660, 375x667 and 360x640, left to right.
- `*_desk.webp`: the desktop target on top, live 1440x900 below.
- `*_other.webp`: live 1920x1080 on top, live 852x393 below.
- `SEASON_ENTRY_*` is the results entry form. `SEASON_REVIEW_*` is the "Final check" stage that follows it.

## Not checked, and why

- **Live site:** unreachable from this container (proxy 403). Main's code was served locally instead; it is the same build.
- **Real Google sign-in and Firebase:** not available here. Signed-in is simulated. Connect Players stays on "Getting your Showdown ready…", so its CHOOSE SEASONS state was not seen. Legacy opened from the Home tile on a fresh profile showed "Your Showdown history could not be loaded / TRY AGAIN" during the flash run, most likely because Firebase is unreachable here; it was not judged.
- **Loser view:** not checked. Final Winner and Season Results were seen as Daniel, the winner; Nik's losing view was not captured. Empty states were only seen in passing during the flash run, with no dimming visible.
- **Other browsers and devices:** Safari, iOS standalone mode and real devices were not tested; everything ran in headless Chromium.
- **Taller phones:** 412x915 and 360x800 were left out on purpose. League's old art there is a known r79 item.
- **Full Motion animations:** judged only by frame brightness and timing, not by eye.
