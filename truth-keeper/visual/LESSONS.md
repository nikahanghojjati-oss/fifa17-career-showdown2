# LESSONS — tooling, process, free-tier boundaries, future research

These are retrospective working lessons rather than instructions to change current production code. Free-tier constraints are scope/intent, not guarantees that any third-party service will remain unlimited or free. Some historical chat titles were not available and are explicitly labeled.

- 2026-08-14 | [lesson] | Source chat: "Title unavailable (developer handoff)" | Source-of-truth handoffs need exact releases, SHAs, fail evidence, next steps and exclusions; broad claims that 'everything is finished' are inadequate without repeatable proof.
- 2026-08-14 | [lesson] | Source chat: "Title unavailable (Save Library identity)" | Identity is a data semantics problem, not just a UI name problem; display-name unification and fresh profile IDs both have failure cases.
