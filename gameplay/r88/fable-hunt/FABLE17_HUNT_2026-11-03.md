## Checked, no bug (so the lead does not re-hunt them)

- **Prime Pack stage points agree between client and Rules.** Client stages (js/iconGuess.js STAGES) start at L+3 s and end at
  27.5 / 49 / 70.5 / 92 s after the start; Rules (`ssjrPackStagePoints`) cut at 30.5 / 52 / 73.5 s after the later arrival L,
  so both give the same N for the same `updatedAt`. Guess window 2 to 97 s after L matches `DRAW_SLACK_MS`. A round with
  `namedBy` set but an arrival missing cannot exist (`igApplyGuess` refuses NOT_STARTED; the Rules' guess write needs both arrivals).
- **Prime Pack points reach every Career screen with one meaning.** `primePack` 1..4 is a sparse key in the result
  (sharedSeasonResults, sparkSharedSeasonResults, sparkSharedSeasonCommit, sharedHistoryConvergence `hcResultKeys`), added to the
  breakdown and total in analytics.js:145, legacy.js:204, sharedHistoryConvergence.js:40, seasonEngine.js:398. Perfect season is 15
  everywhere (sharedCareerAnalytics PERFECT_SEASON, analytics.js:262 `score>=15`, statistics.js "Perfect 15-Point Seasons",
  trophyRoom.js "15 points", careerScreenSeam SEASON_MAX 15, Golden 150). No 11 or 110 left in any screen text.
- **Prime Pack page labels follow Nik's order and names.** "Go to the Transfer War" (page :484, :550), roulette "GO TO THE PRIME
  PACK", Chamber Men "SPIN THE CYLINDER"; no "Transfer Window" or "Transfer Challenge" string in any of the four screens or pages.
  Only a late winner may replace a shown draw (F12-03) and the draw shows 2 s after stage 4 (F13-06).
- **Prime Pack memo.** `igsPointsFor` memoises only a won round; a drawn round costs one transaction read per publish attempt
  (one or two a season). Not worth a change.
- **Chamber Men record and Rules.** Own string only, exactly one round per write, never ahead (`before.kicks[role].size() <=
  before.kicks[other].size()`), 14 characters at most, `[LMR-]{2}`; the client's `cmsSend` binds the pick to its round (Codex
  #1205). A timed-out shot is a miss, a timed-out dive stays in the middle; goal = shot differs from dive; kick k loads chamber k
  of the rival's cylinder; the roulette fires exactly when the stop chamber is live (`rrTurn` with `loaded`), aims where 2.0.0
  aimed (`aimSlot` from the same stream), and a one-bullet Showdown is unchanged. One trigger pull per season, as Nik picked.
- **Chamber Men order and hand-off.** `pstcOpenNow`: Chamber Men, then roulette, then Prime Pack, then the Transfer War; the
  shootout stage (z 95) stays under the roulette (z 96) until the roulette has run, so the dashboard never shows in between
  (CM9). Once the Transfer War has started, Chamber Men and the roulette are marked done and never shown again.
- **Chamber Men screen rules.** Opaque marble stage, no scrim or rgba dimming, `overflow:auto` so every button is reachable on
  a short phone; `.cmBack` has its own handler (smart-back leaves it alone); no `Math.random`. Reus Gate: "LOADING CHAMBER MEN"
  stays until the first record snapshot has drawn the board (`visibility:hidden` until `onData`).
- **Chamber Men clocks.** Rounds 2 to 7 start at the end of this phone's replay on the Transfer Challenge server clock; a phone
  that opens mid-shootout draws finished rounds at once (no replay) and starts its clock now; a late snapshot that is shorter
  than what this phone wrote is ignored. Each phone only ever sends its own pick.
- **Roulette cylinders on the result card.** "Daniel's cylinder held N live rounds of 7 (Nik's Chamber Men goals)" matches the
  scene caption "Daniel N bullets · Nik M bullets · Seven chambers" and the Chamber Men board ("Cylinder: N live" = rival's goals).
  Zero live rounds reads "No live rounds. Every chamber is empty" (`liveWords`), and a saved fired turn with an empty cylinder
  cannot occur (the record is complete and identical on both phones before any spin).
- **Special Player.** Drawn from the rivalry id (hex 9-16 / 17-24), own club skipped from OWN_CLUB_FROM_MS (`mpPicks`), never on
  a roulette board (`specials` exclusion, reason "special"), a typed signing of him is refused (`mpIsOwnSpecial`). Screen words
  are "Special Player" or "SP" only (chip, Gazette, reveal page). The reveal is not marked seen when its page never started
  (R87-SP-03), and the frame's CTA "Opening Career Start…" matches the step after Club Assignment.
- **No tiebreak anywhere in the four screens.** Chamber Men "Level, N-N"; Prime Pack "Nobody got him · 0 points"; the roulette has
  no winner.
