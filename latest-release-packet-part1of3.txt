# Career Mode Showdown — r85: marble music player, premium club crests, safer shared writes (v1.9.9 runtime r85)

Application version: `v1.9.9`  
Runtime asset revision: `1.9.9-r85`  
Previous known-good runtime: `1.9.8-r84`

r85 builds on r84. The application version moves to 1.9.9 and the runtime revision to r85.

## What changed

- Music: nine new songs, with Blur "Song 2" first and John Newman "Love Me Again" second. A marble player sits in the
  middle of the desktop top bar on every screen that has the top bar; Settings has none. On phones it is the MUSIC tab
  in the bottom bar plus Home TRACKS. The tracklist is a readable, keyboard-friendly list with the current song marked
  (#1010, #1018).
- Premium club crests: 98 drawn crests, with no lettering, on Club, Standings, Rivalry and Legacy (#1012).
- Settings SEASONS LEFT TODAY starts from the trimmed season cost (#1011).
- Season acknowledgement race: a second proving read after 400 ms, so a tap made just before the rival's acknowledgement
  is recorded (#1009).
- SSJR test panel (`?ssjr=1`) records the real two-phone run itself, restarts after a closed, failed run, and has
  readable buttons (#1014, #1020).
- Reads counter: direct gets and listeners are counted (#1016).
- Docs: the R84 correction note, current entry points, and scoring flags that say draws (#1015).

## Update safety

- The shell and service worker advance to r85 in a fresh `career-mode-showdown-shell-1.9.9-r85` cache, and r84 is retained
  for rollback. The new cache revision also stops old crests from flashing after the update.

## What did not change

- The scoring formula and the Remote Joining protocol stay as they were. No stored document shape changes. No tiebreak.
- The no-flash rules, the Reus Gate, preload and decode are unchanged.
- Firebase remains Spark-only, billing remains OFF and App Check enforcement remains OFF.
- No saved Showdown is rewritten. Nothing in Firebase is deleted or edited.

## Firestore Rules

- Rules change (audit R84 F01/F02, PR #1017). Every shared write (setup, transfers, results, season commit create and
  acknowledge) now checks the acting account is active, the acting device is registered and active, and the session
  belongs to both members (`ssjrActorWriteAuthorityValid`). The unused `ssjrCommitShape` helper is deleted. Reads stay
  membership-only. Cost: about 28 more billed Rules lookups per season.
- Generated Rules at the release head: `firestore.spark.generated.rules`, 123,027 bytes, SHA-256
  `b6a3206e0b1f4296a12450e7670feba20e5902e727aa8e996b7ef9fa6cf20f19`.
- Rollback pair: rolling back only the app does not roll back the Rules. The r84 rollback pair is the r84 app with the
  r84 generated Rules (SHA-256 `36d58d21147004f3c768cdadb219c4617f78d0c312b74207b6606844cc98b131`).
