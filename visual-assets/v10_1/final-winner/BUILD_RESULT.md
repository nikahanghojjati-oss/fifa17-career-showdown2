## Mockup changes required by product truth

The Trophy Room plate, cinematic champion composition, centre trophy, brush title and bottom result strip are retained. The mockup's season-entry and scoring controls were removed because Final Winner is read-only. Only the Terminal Close actions recorded by product truth are mounted; completion-hub buttons are not invented.

Equal total Showdown points render a balanced draw with no crown. A confirmed winner receives the warm side spotlight while the rival remains visible at about 70 percent. Completion-pending stays explicit. Missing authoritative history values render as unavailable dashes rather than invented zeroes. There is no bottom navigation bar on this screen.

The art is original Showdown material only: the approved Trophy Room-derived plate, Showdown Champion wordmark, Showdown trophy family and the recipe-only manager arm/rim overlays.
