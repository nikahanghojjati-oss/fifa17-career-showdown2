# Crest closeness rules V1 (2026-09-30)

Nik's direction (2026-09-30): crests and league marks should look as close to the real ones as possible without copying them. This builds on ROADMAP_AMENDMENTS.md §2 and js/visualIdentity.js. It is not a rule change: official crests stay out.

## What each crest may share with the real badge
1. **Colours.** Always allowed: the club's real colours and kit pattern (stripes, halves).
2. **One theme.** Choose one, drawn from scratch: the club's animal, object or heritage pattern (cannon, fork, bird, lion, crown, lozenges, stripes).

## What must always differ
- **Outline.** If the real badge is a roundel, ours is a shield, and the other way round.
- **Pose and direction** of the theme (e.g. the cannon faces the other way, the bird flies instead of standing, a lion head instead of a full lion).
- **Layout.** No copied rings, scrolls, bands or quarters in the same places.
- **Text.** No club name, initials monogram, founding year or motto baked into the crest. The name shows as live text beside it.
- Never trace, redraw or edit the real artwork or its vector paths.

## League marks
Rounded gold-rim tile in the league colour, holding the national flag as a small shield (Nik, 2026-09-30), a crown or star, and a country code (ENG · I). No league wordmark, lion, player silhouette or rooster.

## Check before shipping a crest
Someone who knows the club should think "those are their colours", not "that's their badge". If a crest passes as the real one at a glance, pull it back one step.
