# USER-SHARED SCREENSHOT STUDY — 2026-09-27

The screenshots were used as scouting input, not authority.

## Screenshot: VoiceStudio post

Useful visual lesson from the embedded product preview:
- dark product can feel premium when type, glow, color accents and feature grouping are restrained and coherent;
- central brand statement is stronger than a generic dashboard header;
- card surfaces can sit inside a larger atmospheric composition rather than becoming the whole composition.

Do not copy the product or its exact styles.

## Screenshots: "28 vibe coding resources" thread

V5 policy:
- use these as scouting/reference discovery when needed;
- do not make template/prompt galleries the foundation of Showdown;
- do not paste a fashionable component into the product without reconciling it with Showdown's type, spacing, material and interaction system.

Reason:
A library of attractive components can increase local polish while destroying global product coherence.
