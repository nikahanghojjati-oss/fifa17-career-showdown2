Classification: REFERENCE_ONLY
Generation requested: NO
Use: cinematic depth, football-game feeling, staging, environmental integration

## Overall finding

The strongest images do not feel premium because they have more gold or more objects.

They feel premium because they create a believable spatial event.

Across the set, the repeated high-value structure is:

foreground interruption -> character plane -> dominant object / live board -> stadium architecture -> crowd / lights / sky

That layering creates scale, parallax potential, occlusion, depth and game-world presence.

## V10 depth rules derived from the set

1. Use foreground occlusion intentionally on Level-A screens.
2. Give dominant objects physical scale and contact with the scene.
3. Preserve at least three readable depth planes on desktop.
4. Characters must receive light from the same world as the UI/props.
5. Use perspective/leading lines to guide attention toward the live task.
6. Diegetic screens/boards may hold live DOM overlays, but raster art must never bake volatile product truth.
7. Atmosphere should reveal depth, not merely darken the screen.
8. Gold practical lights should have believable sources.
9. Allow controlled asymmetry and partial cropping; perfect centered-card grids often flatten the scene.
10. Mobile preserves game feel using environment crop, lighting, material and dominant-object metaphor even when large hero characters disappear.

## Artifact-error filter

Because these are generated concept references, ignore:
- extra/incorrect hands;
- impossible props;
- unreadable/generated labels;
- character-identity drift;
- fake brand/league content;
- impossible perspective joins;
- stale functionality.

V10 learns the art direction, not the accidental generated details.
