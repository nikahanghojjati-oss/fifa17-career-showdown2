## Method

Independent source-level resources in this ledger: 284
This is a source-level research corpus, not a claim that 200 paid courses or books were completed cover-to-cover.

Study tiers:
- D = substantive/deep review of the accessible page, chapter structure, case study or official guidance.
- T = targeted review of the relevant official section/page/material.
- B = breadth scan used for comparative pattern mapping.

Paywalled/private course material is never represented as completed. Public curriculum structures may be studied as curriculum evidence.

The owner-provided inspiration screenshots and their resource-list thread are NOT counted toward this independent corpus.
